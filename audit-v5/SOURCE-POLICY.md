# Source Policy

Version: `source-1-draft`

Source preference:

1. TWSE / TPEx / MOPS / MOEA / official regulator or government source
2. formal company financial statements, annual reports, investor presentations and material disclosures
3. official company pages/product pages
4. high-quality industry/news sources
5. other secondary sources
6. search snippets as leads only

High-risk claims should preferentially be supported by levels 1-3.

A current undated webpage cannot silently prove a historical-period claim. Source publication/effective period must be bound or the item remains period-unresolved/partially supported.

Inaccessible source, unsupported PDF, or unreliable OCR remains unresolved. Never convert inability to inspect into factual acceptance.
