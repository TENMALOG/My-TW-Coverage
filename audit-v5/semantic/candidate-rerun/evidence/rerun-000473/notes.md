# 重驗筆記 — 岱宇 / 1598:objective_ranking_or_market_share:e02e4b48f7e5db2a

原始 claim：[[Dick's Sporting Goods]] (美國最大合作夥伴)、[[Academy Sports]]

## 原子主張

1. `UNKNOWN` — DICK'S Sporting Goods is Dyaco's largest U.S. partner.
   期間：Not specified in the claim; no ranking period established by the evidence.
   說明：Dyaco's 2020 presentation documents SOLE sales through DICK'S online store, but does not rank DICK'S among Dyaco's partners or customers. A 2017 issuer prospectus describes a major North American customer as contributing about 30% of revenue without identifying it. That disclosure cannot establish that the customer was DICK'S or that DICK'S was the largest U.S. partner; the ranking's definition and denominator are also unspecified.

   來源：[岱宇國際股份有限公司公開說明書 (Dyaco International Inc. prospectus)](https://www.cathaysec.com.tw/RiskManage/0203F6CA-2D3C-4E4A-ADD3-3ECE645B7DF8.pdf)；定位：Section 9, sales-concentration risk

   期間：2020, as described in Dyaco's issuer presentation.
   說明：The issuer presentation specifically identifies DICK'S online store as a sales channel for SOLE. This supports a historical brand/channel relationship, not a ranking of DICK'S among Dyaco's partners or customers.
   來源：[Dyaco International Inc. Company Presentation (issuer-hosted English PDF)](https://dyaco.com/getmedia/f4f81000-7649-47e1-8f01-bf9bcb7a29fd/%E5%B2%B1%E5%AE%87-1598-%E5%85%AC%E5%8F%B8%E7%B0%A1%E5%A0%B1-%E8%8B%B1%E6%96%87%E7%89%88-0106%E6%9B%B4.pdf)；定位：Slide 9, SOLE brand discussion
3. `SUPPORTED` — Academy Sports was a U.S. retail channel for Dyaco's XTERRA products.
   期間：2013, as reported in the source.
   說明：The report says XTERRA products had entered the U.S. Academy Sports retail channel in 2013. It does not establish a current relationship or quantify sales, share, or the scope of any formal partnership.
   來源：[岱宇明年全球營運待發 搶攻歐、亞家用健身市場 — 鉅亨網](https://news.cnyes.com/news/id/2064471)；定位：Article paragraph describing the XTERRA brand's U.S. channels

## 判定

Overall `PARTIAL`。

建議改寫：Historical evidence indicates that Dyaco's SOLE brand was sold through DICK'S online store in 2020 and its XTERRA products had entered Academy Sports in the U.S. in 2013. The evidence does not establish that DICK'S was Dyaco's largest U.S. partner.

來源限制：
- The claim provides no date or definition for “largest partner” (for example, by revenue, units, or strategic importance).
- The issuer's 2017 customer-concentration disclosure does not identify the customer; it must not be attributed to DICK'S without evidence.
- Evidence for Academy Sports is historical and does not establish that the relationship continued after 2013.

Focused review of issuer-hosted disclosures and Taiwanese financial-news reporting found support for historical sales-channel relationships, but no evidence identifying DICK'S as Dyaco's largest U.S. partner. The ranking remains unresolved.
