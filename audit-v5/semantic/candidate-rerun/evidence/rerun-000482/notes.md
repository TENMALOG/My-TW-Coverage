## 判定

Overall `PARTIAL`。

建議改寫：美利達在歐洲設有經銷商網絡；一份2026年8月的法說會摘要稱，歐洲約占集團營收55%，為其最大區域市場。現有資料未確認該網絡皆為自行車專賣店，也未量化其歐洲整體規模。

來源限制：
- The original phrase does not specify whether “largest market” refers to MERIDA's regional sales or to the overall bicycle market; the evidence found supports only the former interpretation.
- The European revenue-share evidence is secondary and does not specify the exact measurement period.
- Official distributor materials confirm distributor/dealer presence but do not verify specialty-shop composition or total European network size.
