## 判定

Overall `PARTIAL`。

建議改寫：TaiHan's FY2024 annual report identifies Brother as its largest named sales customer, at 59.91% of net sales. Historical sources reported Canon, Epson, Brother, and Kyocera as principal Japanese printer/MFP brand customers around 2020–2021; current status for Canon, Epson, and Kyocera, and the claim's unspecified additional brands, remains unverified.

來源限制：
- The FY2024 annual report identifies Brother but aggregates other sales as “Other”; that aggregation does not prove the other named brands were absent.
- The issuer's annual-report page lists a 2025 Annual Report, but its customer table was not retrieved, leaving the latest-period customer mix unresolved.
- The 2020–2021 customer-name reporting is secondary and historical; it cannot by itself substantiate an undated present-tense relationship.

Stopped within the focused-research limit after reviewing issuer annual-report disclosures and historical corroboration. The 2025 annual report is listed by the issuer, but its customer disclosure was not retrieved; current-period status therefore remains unresolved.
