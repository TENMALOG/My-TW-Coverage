## 判定

Overall `PARTIAL`。

建議改寫：Issuer disclosures identify Honda, Yamaha, and Ducati as customers. Honda was the largest customer in the issuer’s reported customer-structure data through 2018 Q3; the current or otherwise unqualified largest-customer ranking is not verified.

來源限制：
- The issuer’s historical customer-structure table reports customer shares as a percentage of revenue and ends at 2018 Q3; it cannot establish a current ranking.
- The contemporary secondary article asserting Honda is largest does not state the ranking period or customer-revenue denominator.
- Issuer customer lists establish named relationships but do not quantify Ducati’s share or rank it.

Focused searches located issuer presentations and company disclosures confirming the customer relationships and historical Honda ranking. The unqualified/current Honda ranking remains unresolved because available evidence does not bind it to a current period and denominator.
