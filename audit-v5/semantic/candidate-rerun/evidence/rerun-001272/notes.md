## 判定

Overall `PARTIAL`。

建議改寫：CPC Corporation, Taiwan was Hsin-Kao Gas’s principal reported natural-gas supplier in the periods disclosed: it supplied 99.95% of reported net purchases in 2023 and 2024, and 99.96% through 2025 Q1. The report also lists a small “other” share, so exclusivity is not supported for those periods; exclusivity after 2025 Q1 remains unverified.

來源限制：
- The annual report presents supplier shares by net purchase amount, in NT$ thousands; it does not present shares by physical gas volume.
- The “other” supplier category is not individually identified, and the cited report does not establish who it comprises.
- The company product page is undated; its mention of historical alternative procurement and reciprocal support agreements does not establish actual current supply from those counterparties.

Focused searches located the issuer’s official annual report with supplier data through 2025 Q1 and its official undated product page, but no later dated supplier evidence resolving exclusivity as of October 7, 2026.
