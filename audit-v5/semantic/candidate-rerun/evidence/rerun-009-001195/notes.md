## 判定

Overall `PARTIAL`。

建議改寫：IET's 2015 presentation described a historical supply relationship with Skyworks: IET said it took over Skyworks' MBE production equipment in 2014 and exclusively supplied certain non-mobile high-frequency epitaxial wafers. Available evidence does not establish that Skyworks was a major customer in the claim's unspecified period.

來源限制：
- Historical supply evidence is not evidence that the relationship continued or remained material in later periods.
- The annual report's top-customer percentage is a share of total sales, but the customer is unnamed; it cannot be attributed to Skyworks.
- Application-market revenue mix is not customer revenue or customer rank.

Reviewed issuer presentations, the issuer's supplier-award announcement, and the annual report. The historical relationship is supported, but the claim's period and the basis for calling Skyworks a major customer remain unresolved.
