## 判定

Overall `UNKNOWN`。

建議改寫：If the intended subject is caprolactam, available official disclosures support that it is an important or starting raw material for Nylon 6 products, including fiber and engineering-plastic grades; they do not establish that it is the sole raw material.

來源限制：
- The original wording omits the subject; CPL is inferred from the issuer’s product context.
- The official issuer pages describe CPL as an “important” raw material and nylon chips as its downstream derivatives, not as the sole raw material.
- The BASF release uses “starting material,” which does not establish exclusivity.

Focused research found relevant official issuer and manufacturer disclosures for the CPL-to-PA6 relationship, but none resolved the exclusivity wording. The unresolved atoms remain UNKNOWN rather than treating absence of an exclusivity statement as evidence against the claim.
