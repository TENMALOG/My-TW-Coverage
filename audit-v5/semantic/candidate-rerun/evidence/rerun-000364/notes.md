Overall `PARTIAL`。

來源限制：
- The issuer's annual report confirms the named brands but does not provide brand-level sales, procurement, or shipment comparisons to establish a largest line.
- Company-wide revenue mix is not a substitute for brand-level ranking; no such mix was used to support the largest-line qualifier.
- The original wording does not specify the ranking period, geographic scope, metric, or denominator.

Focused searches of issuer annual reports, ESG and product-line disclosures, and ranking-related queries found official support for the named agency brands but no official, scoped measure establishing AMD as the largest agency line. The ranking remains UNKNOWN.
