MoneyDJ describes 鼎固 as an ASE Group investee in mainland China, but the formal parent-company relationship is not established by the sources reviewed.

來源限制：

Eight focused research turns were used. The OSAT ranking is supported within the stated scope; the exact 鼎固-KY parent-company relationship remains unresolved.
