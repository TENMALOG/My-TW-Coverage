## 判定

Overall `PARTIAL`。

建議改寫：In its 2025 investor presentation, 大魯閣 stated that it operated 10 batting venues, 3 bowling venues, and 1 combined batting-and-bowling venue. The presentation supports that batting facilities are part of its business, but does not establish the claim’s undated count of 12 batting venues or its Taiwan-wide exclusivity and largest-size rankings.

來源限制：
- The original wording does not specify an effective date for either venue count or the ranking claims.
- The issuer’s 2025 count distinguishes dedicated batting and bowling venues from a combined venue; treating the combined location as a batting venue or a bowling alley changes the denominator.
- No comparable market definition or size metric was provided for the batting-chain ranking. Company marketing about its bowling chain is not evidence of a batting-chain ranking.

Completed eight focused search calls. The remaining ranking claims and the original reference period for the counts could not be reliably established.
