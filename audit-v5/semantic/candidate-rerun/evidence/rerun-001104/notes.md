## 判定

Overall `CANDIDATE_ACCEPTED`。

建議改寫：Ajinomoto's ABF is a film-type interlayer insulating material for advanced semiconductor package substrates. Ajinomoto estimates its global share at 95% or higher in the FY2022 high-performance-substrate interlayer-insulating-film market; a March 2026 company article separately reports 95% for FC-BGA applications.

來源限制：
- The market-share figures are Ajinomoto Group estimates or statements; the sources do not provide an independent audit or detailed methodology.
- The FY2022 report defines the relevant market as interlayer insulating film for high-performance semiconductor substrates. The March 2026 article refers to FC-BGA applications and does not fully specify its denominator.
- The reviewed sources establish the ABF product and Ajinomoto's reported global market position; they do not independently establish a specific supplier-customer relationship between Ajinomoto and Nan Ya PCB.

Official Ajinomoto sources established the product identity, function, and near-exclusive global share in the relevant advanced-substrate applications. Further research was not needed to support the wording as written; issuer-specific purchasing and independent market verification remain caveats.
