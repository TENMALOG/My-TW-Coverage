# 重驗筆記 — 世芯-KY / 3661:objective_ranking_or_market_share:3565731645cc816a

原始 claim：**合作代工**: [[台積電]] (先進製程唯一夥伴)。

## 原子主張

1. `SUPPORTED` — TSMC is an advanced-process partner/foundry for Alchip.
   期間：Alchip's official page describes TSMC 3nm collaboration and says its first 3nm test chip taped out in January 2023; no broader claim period is specified.
   說明：Alchip identifies itself as a founding member of TSMC's OIP 3DFabric Alliance using TSMC 3nm process technology. This supports the TSMC advanced-process relationship, but does not establish that TSMC is the sole foundry across all Alchip advanced-process work.
   來源：[Advanced Package Solutions - Alchip](https://www.alchip.com/en/ASIC_Turnkey_Solutions/ASIC_Design_Services/Advanced_Package_Solutions)；定位：Opening paragraph and TSMC 3DFabric technology discussion
2. `UNKNOWN` — TSMC is Alchip's only partner for advanced processes.
   期間：Unspecified in the claim and not established by the sources reviewed.
   說明：The official disclosures reviewed confirm TSMC collaboration but do not say it is exclusive or define the full set of Alchip's advanced-process foundry partners. Evidence of a TSMC relationship cannot establish that no other partner exists.
   來源：[Advanced Package Solutions - Alchip](https://www.alchip.com/en/ASIC_Turnkey_Solutions/ASIC_Design_Services/Advanced_Package_Solutions)；定位：Opening paragraph and TSMC 3DFabric technology discussion
   來源：[Alchip Technologies, Limited 2024 Annual Report](https://www.alchip.com/upload/2025_05_15/3_20250515095519oqqpsf9j21.pdf)；定位：I. Letter to Shareholders; IV. Overview of Business Operations

## 判定

Overall `PARTIAL`。

建議改寫：Alchip publicly identifies TSMC as a partner for 3nm process and 3DFabric work; the reviewed evidence does not establish that TSMC is its only advanced-process partner.

來源限制：
- The direct evidence is an issuer disclosure of collaboration and process use, not an explicit statement of exclusive foundry status.
- The annual report is an English summary translation and states that the Chinese version prevails.

Focused searches of Alchip's official disclosures and its 2024 annual report supported the TSMC relationship but did not resolve exclusivity; the sole-partner atom remains UNKNOWN.
