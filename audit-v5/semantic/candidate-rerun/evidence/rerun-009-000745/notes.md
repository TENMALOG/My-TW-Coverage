建議改寫：亞泰金屬在其2023年年報中表示，該公司是全球最大的高階含浸設備製造商，全球市佔率超過70%；該數字由公司自述，年報未說明計算分母或方法，且未將此市佔率明確擴及塗佈設備。

來源限制：
- The >70% figure is an issuer statement, not an independently verified market measurement.
- The issuer's stated scope is advanced impregnation equipment; evidence does not support applying the figure to coating equipment or to a combined equipment market.
- The source does not specify the market-share denominator, measurement unit, methodology, or exact reference date.

Official issuer materials support the narrower advanced-impregnation share claim, but not the broader combined impregnation-and-coating share. Further attribution or product descriptions would not resolve the missing market scope and denominator.
