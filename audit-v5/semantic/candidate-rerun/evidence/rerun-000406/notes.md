## 判定

Overall `PARTIAL`。

建議改寫：SVP Worldwide identifies SINGER as one of its brands. In an October 2024 report, UAnalyze named SINGER the leading brand by estimated share in the global household sewing-machine market (30–33%); this does not establish SINGER’s standalone ranking across the broader sewing-machine market.

來源限制：
- The official SVP Worldwide statement concerns the company and its three-brand portfolio, not SINGER as a standalone brand.
- The market-share ranking comes from secondary analysis, not an issuer or regulator disclosure; its calculation method, precise denominator, and underlying measurement period are not specified.
- The ranking evidence is limited to household sewing machines, whereas the original wording does not define the market segment.

Focused research found official evidence for the SINGER–SVP brand relationship and secondary evidence for a household-market ranking, but did not establish SINGER’s standalone first-place share across all sewing-machine categories. The broader ranking remains UNKNOWN.
