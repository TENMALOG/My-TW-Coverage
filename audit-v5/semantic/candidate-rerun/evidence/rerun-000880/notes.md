## 判定

Overall `PARTIAL`。

建議改寫：A secondary summary of Hota's November 14, 2024 investor conference reports Tesla as Hota's second-largest customer for January–October 2024 and lists 20%, without specifying the percentage denominator. A trade report circa 2014 attributed to Hota's chairman the statement that Hota was Tesla's sole reduction-gearbox supplier; current exclusivity and product scope remain unverified.

來源限制：
- The customer-rank and 20% figures come from an individual investor's secondary conference notes, not a verified issuer transcript.
- The 20% denominator and unit are unspecified.
- The sole-supplier evidence is historical secondary reporting and does not establish a current, Tesla-wide exclusive relationship.
- An official Taiwan Stock Exchange recording of Hota's November 14, 2024 investor conference was located, but its substantive contents were not available in searchable text for this review.

Focused searches found secondary reporting for the 2024 customer rank/share and historical exclusivity, but no accessible primary evidence resolving the 20% denominator or current exclusivity and scope. Further searches did not resolve those atoms; they remain UNKNOWN.
