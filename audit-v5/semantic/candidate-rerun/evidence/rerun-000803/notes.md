## 判定

Overall `PARTIAL`。

建議改寫：Inmax's operating-business lineage traces to Inmax Sdn. Bhd., established in Malaysia in 1989; formal production at a leased Malaysian factory began in May 1990. The Cayman-incorporated Inmax Holding parent was established in 2009. The group manufactures pneumatic industrial nails, including special-steel nail products. The issuer has described itself as Southeast Asia's largest supplier, but available evidence does not independently establish a region-wide largest-manufacturer ranking or its denominator.

來源限制：
- The issuer's chronology separates the 1989 establishment of its Malaysian operating subsidiary from the 1990 start of formal factory production and the 2009 establishment of the Cayman parent.
- The largest-ranking statements are issuer and secondary-source descriptions; no comparative dataset, defined market denominator, units, or method substantiating the ranking was located.

Reviewed the issuer's annual-report chronology and product description, its current company introduction, and focused searches for a comparative Southeast Asian ranking or market denominator. The available ranking material remains descriptive rather than measurable, so the ranking atom remains UNKNOWN.
