   期間：2017
   說明：The CNA report says the company had more than half of the orders at CTBC, Cathay United, and Taishin, and had cooperated with E.Sun in the past. This is historical reporting, not evidence of current customer status.
   來源：[台灣信用卡製卡霸主 台灣銘板11月上櫃](https://tw.stock.yahoo.com/news/%E5%8F%B0%E7%81%A3%E4%BF%A1%E7%94%A8%E5%8D%A1%E8%A3%BD%E5%8D%A1%E9%9C%B8%E4%B8%BB-%E5%8F%B0%E7%81%A3%E9%8A%98%E6%9D%BF11%E6%9C%88%E4%B8%8A%E6%AB%83-095440218.html)；定位：Article paragraph beginning「台灣銘板表示，國內4大信用卡發卡行」

## 判定

Overall `PARTIAL`。

建議改寫：In 2017 press coverage, Taiwan Numerical Powers was reported to serve three of Taiwan's four major credit-card issuers. A contemporaneous report cited a 35% market-share figure in the context of chip credit cards, but its denominator and applicability to debit cards are unverified.

來源限制：
- The available customer and issuer-ranking evidence is secondary reporting from 2017 and should not be treated as current.
- The 35% figure is reported without a defined denominator or precise measurement period; no evidence located establishes a chip debit-card market share.

Focused searches located contemporaneous secondary reporting but no reliable primary source that defines or independently verifies the 35% market-share denominator, period, or debit-card scope.
