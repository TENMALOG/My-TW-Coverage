# 重驗筆記 — 長榮航太 / 2645:material_named_relationship:8a2772f0488a5e3a

原始 claim：製造端為[[Boeing]]與[[Airbus]]之 Tier 1 供應商，2025 年正式升格為[[Airbus]]一階機體零組件直供商。

## 原子主張

1. `SUPPORTED` — 長榮航太製造端是 Boeing 供應商。
   期間：Established by company and Boeing-related sources through 2025; Boeing supplier recognition dated 2022.
   說明：Boeing identifies EGAT as a supplier and lists rigid cargo barriers, main deck cargo doors, and sub-assemblies as associated products. A 2025 report also identifies Boeing as an existing manufacturing customer. These establish the supplier relationship, but not by themselves its tier.
   來源：[Evergreen Aviation Technologies Corp. — Boeing Supplier Spotlight](https://www.boeing.com/content/dam/boeing/boeingdotcom/suppliers/Evergreen-Aviation-Technologies-Corp.pdf)；定位：Page 1, supplier tenure and Boeing-associated products.
   來源：[長榮航太打入空巴產業鏈 列一階機體組件供應商](https://www.cna.com.tw/news/afe/202505090267.aspx)；定位：Paragraphs 277–280.
2. `UNKNOWN` — 長榮航太製造端是 Boeing 的 Tier 1 供應商。
   期間：Claim has no specified start date or model/program scope; sources document Boeing supplier activity through 2025.
   說明：Boeing and company-related sources confirm supplier status and products, while an industry-yearbook source discusses Tier-1 system-parts capability alongside Boeing orders. The reviewed sources do not explicitly establish that EGAT’s Boeing contracts themselves are Tier 1. A general Tier-1 classification for manufacturing is not enough to bind that tier to Boeing specifically.
   來源：[Evergreen Aviation Technologies Corp. — Boeing Supplier Spotlight](https://www.boeing.com/content/dam/boeing/boeingdotcom/suppliers/Evergreen-Aviation-Technologies-Corp.pdf)；定位：Page 1, supplier tenure and Boeing-associated products.
   來源：[112-113年工業發展年鑑, 第二篇金屬機電產業篇, 第四章重機產業](https://www.ida.gov.tw/ctlr?PRO=filepath.DownloadFile&f=publication&id=3474&t=f)；定位：Search-indexed passage on EGAT’s Tier-1 system-parts supply-chain capability and Boeing 777 RCB/MDCD orders.
3. `SUPPORTED` — 長榮航太於 2025 年成為 Airbus 機體組件的一階（Tier 1）供應商。
   期間：2025-05-09 announcement.
   說明：CNA reports the company announced completion of a cooperation agreement with Airbus and became a Tier 1 supplier for airframe components. This supports the Airbus-specific tier claim.
   來源：[長榮航太打入空巴產業鏈 列一階機體組件供應商](https://www.cna.com.tw/news/afe/202505090267.aspx)；定位：Paragraphs 277–280.
4. `SUPPORTED` — 長榮航太於 2025 年正式建立對 Airbus 機體零組件的直接供應關係。
   期間：By 2025-05-19.
   說明：The issuer’s May 2025 investor presentation says the Airbus agreement was completed and components would be supplied directly to Airbus’s production line. CNA separately describes the resulting Tier 1 airframe-component supplier status.
   來源：[長榮航太法人說明會, 2025-05-19](https://www.egat.com.tw/documents/295/PPTfashuohuai519chinese.pdf)；定位：營運策略 slide, bullet stating the Airbus agreement was completed and components would be supplied directly to the Airbus production line.
   來源：[長榮航太打入空巴產業鏈 列一階機體組件供應商](https://www.cna.com.tw/news/afe/202505090267.aspx)；定位：Paragraphs 277–280.

## 判定

Overall `PARTIAL`。

建議改寫：長榮航太製造端為 Boeing 供應商；2025 年與 Airbus 完成合作合約簽署，成為 Airbus 機體組件 Tier 1 供應商，並直接供應組件至空巴產線。現有證據未能明確確認其 Boeing 合約的 Tier 1 層級。

來源限制：
- Boeing supplier status and Boeing-specific Tier 1 status are distinct: the former is supported; the latter is not explicitly established by the located sources.
- The Airbus Tier 1 characterization is reported by CNA; the issuer’s own investor presentation independently confirms the signed agreement and direct component supply to the Airbus production line.
- No market denominator or sales measure applies to these relationship/status claims.

Focused research found strong issuer and news support for the 2025 Airbus relationship, but did not resolve whether EGAT’s Boeing contracts are specifically Tier 1. Further review is left to final adjudication.
