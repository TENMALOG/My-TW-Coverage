# 重驗筆記 — 泓辰 / 3616:objective_ranking_or_market_share:da6309bf6fbd75aa

原始 claim：泓辰材料 (3616，HCM Co.) 成立於 2001 年，總部位於桃園市平鎮區，是全球少數具備[[磷酸錳鐵鋰]] ([[LMFP]]) 正極材料量產能力的企業，亦為全球唯一「非中國」之 [[LMFP]] 正極材料製造商。

## 原子主張

1. `SUPPORTED` — The company was founded in 2001.
   期間：2001
   說明：The official TPEX company profile states that the corporate entity was established in Minguo year 90 (2001). It notes the entity then used the name 宏瀨科技 and changed its name to 泓辰材料 in 2020; this supports the founding year of the entity, not the current name being used in 2001.
   來源：[公司概況資料表（泓辰材料股份有限公司，股票代號 3616）](https://www.tpex.org.tw/storage/emerging_register/2024/10/1730081425_9960_CH_3616.pdf)；定位：歷史沿革：民國104年總公司3月遷址至新北市中和、同年7月搬遷至桃園市平鎮工業區。
2. `SUPPORTED` — The company is headquartered in Taoyuan City’s Pingzhen District.
   期間：Listed as the company address on the undated official site; TPEX company history records the move to Pingzhen in 2015.
   說明：HCM’s official site lists its address in Pingzhen, Taoyuan City. The TPEX profile records the head-office move to the Pingzhen industrial area in 2015.
   來源：[HCM 泓辰 — official website](https://www.hcmaterial.com.tw/en)；定位：Contact/address section: No. 2-3, Gongye 1st Rd., Pingzhen Dist., Taoyuan City.
   來源：[公司概況資料表（泓辰材料股份有限公司，股票代號 3616）](https://www.tpex.org.tw/storage/emerging_register/2024/10/1730081425_9960_CH_3616.pdf)；定位：歷史沿革：民國104年總公司3月遷址至新北市中和、同年7月搬遷至桃園市平鎮工業區。
3. `SUPPORTED` — HCM has LMFP cathode-material mass-production capability.
   期間：HCM says mass production began in 2016; HCM reported its production line fully operating in July 2025.
   說明：HCM’s own site says it put LMFP into mass production in 2016, and its July 2025 issuer news says its production line was fully operating and had expansion capacity. These are issuer statements, not independent verification of production volume or capacity.
   來源：[HCM — About](https://www.hcmaterial.com.tw/en/about)；定位：About HCM: company self-describes as the first to mass-produce LMFP.
   來源：[HCM 穩定供應全球 LMFP 材料](https://www.hcmaterial.com.tw/tw/news/page/HCM%20%E7%A9%A9%E5%AE%9A%E4%BE%9B%E6%87%89%E5%85%A8%E7%90%83%20LMFP%20%E6%9D%90%E6%96%99%EF%BD%9C2025%20%E5%B9%B4%207%20%E6%9C%88)；定位：Article states that the production line was fully started, with capacity for expansion and flexible supply.
4. `UNKNOWN` — HCM is one of the few companies globally with LMFP cathode-material mass-production capability.
   期間：Not specified in the claim; no complete global comparison for a defined date was established.
   說明：HCM supports its own mass-production capability, but “one of the few” has no defined count, market boundary, or date. The evidence reviewed does not establish a reliable global census of companies with operating LMFP mass-production capability.
   來源：[HCM — About](https://www.hcmaterial.com.tw/en/about)；定位：About HCM: company self-describes as the first to mass-produce LMFP.
5. `UNKNOWN` — HCM is the world’s only non-China LMFP cathode-material manufacturer.
   期間：Not specified; exclusivity is not established for a defined date or manufacturer/production threshold.
   說明：No reliable global census establishes this exclusivity. ARENA described VSPC in Australia as planning a 250-tonne-per-year demonstration facility to produce LFP and LMFP powders and supply samples for future commercial customers. That is evidence of non-China LMFP development, but not evidence that another non-China manufacturer already had an operating mass-production facility; it therefore does not by itself contradict the claim under a mass-production definition.
   來源：[ARENA funds breakthrough battery cathode technology project](https://arena.gov.au/assets/2025/02/ARENA-Media-Release_VSPC_20022025_FINAL.pdf)；定位：Media release: VSPC project to construct a 250 tonne per annum demonstration facility; process produces LFP and LMFP powders; samples intended for potential offtake partners and investors, with a future commercial plant as the goal.

## 判定

Overall `PARTIAL`。

建議改寫：The corporate entity was established in 2001 under a different name and renamed 泓辰材料 in 2020. HCM’s official site lists an address in Pingzhen, Taoyuan, and says it began LMFP mass production in 2016; it reported an operating production line in July 2025. Its global rarity and status as the sole non-China LMFP manufacturer remain unverified.

來源限制：
- The TPEX company profile is an official trading-center disclosure but identifies the company and recommending securities firms as responsible for the supplied information.
- HCM’s production history and operating-line statements are issuer disclosures; the reviewed material does not independently verify output volumes or capacity.
- The words “few,” “only,” “manufacturer,” and “mass-production capability” are not tied in the claim to a date, a defined production threshold, or an exhaustive global market denominator.

Focused research supported the founding year, listed Pingzhen address, and HCM’s self-reported LMFP production capability. The comparative rarity and sole non-China assertions remain unresolved because no reliable, time-bounded global census or consistent definition of operating production was found.
