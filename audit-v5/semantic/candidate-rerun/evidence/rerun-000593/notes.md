## 判定

Overall `PARTIAL`。

建議改寫：穩懋在2026年公司簡報中自稱為全球第一的化合物半導體晶圓代工服務供應商；現有證據未能確認其市佔率超過70%。

來源限制：
- The global ranking is supported by an issuer statement, not an independently verified market ranking.
- The share figures located concern GaAs foundries, not a clearly defined market covering all compound-semiconductor foundry services.
- No reliable source located defines and verifies a greater-than-70% share with a matching period and denominator.

Focused searches checked current issuer materials, historical issuer disclosures, and secondary coverage. The ranking is supported as an issuer assertion, but the market-share atom remains unresolved because no source located supplies the required scope, period, denominator, and greater-than-70% measurement.
