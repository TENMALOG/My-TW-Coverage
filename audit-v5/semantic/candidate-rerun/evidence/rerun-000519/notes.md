## 判定

Overall `PARTIAL`。

建議改寫：大華金屬的官網稱其在國內鋁罐市場市占率超過三成、位居產業龍頭；MoneyDJ於2025-07-17的公司資料稱其為國內最大鋁罐製造商，並稱市占率約35%，但該百分比的期間與計算口徑未披露。MoneyDJ亦稱大華為國內唯一綜合性包裝容器製造企業；其「綜合性」分類標準未說明。

來源限制：
- MoneyDJ is a secondary source and dates its entry update, not the effective period of the ranking or market-share estimate.
- The issuer website is primary for the company's own statements, but its leadership and over-30% share claims are self-reported and lack a cited methodology.
- No source located defined the denominator for the approximately 35% share or the criteria for a comprehensive packaging-container manufacturer.

Focused searches found matching secondary reporting and a current issuer website disclosure, but did not locate an official annual-report or regulator source validating the exclusivity claim or the approximately 35% figure with a defined period and denominator. The unresolved market-share scope prevents full support.
