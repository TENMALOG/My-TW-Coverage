## 判定

Overall `PARTIAL`。

建議改寫：台塑企業透過煉油、輕油裂解及下游石化與塑膠生產，形成跨上下游的整合布局；企業內不同公司生產乙烯、丙烯、PVC、PE、PP等產品。台灣塑膠工業公司另有碳纖維及高吸水性樹脂（SAP）產品。

來源限制：
- Claim text uses 台塑 ambiguously; issuer 1301 and 台塑企業 are not interchangeable. The upstream feedstocks and PP production cited are allocated across different group companies.
- The ranking phrase lacks a comparison year, defined peer set, and measurement basis. A secondary profile’s unsupported ranking statement does not resolve those omissions.
- The reviewed sources establish selected operations and products, not a bounded, fully complete industry chain.
- The official product pages verify carbon fiber and SAP, but do not clearly classify both together as specialty chemicals.

Focused research found official evidence for group-level integration and the named products, but no authoritative, method-defined ranking for the original prominence claim and no defined scope for “complete industry chain.” Further evidence would need a comparable ranking basis and a precise chain boundary.
