## 判定

Overall `PARTIAL`。

建議改寫：台灣福興工業股份有限公司於1957年成立，營運總部位於台灣高雄市。公司自述為全球門控安全科技系統的領導者；其2023年永續報告稱其為全球最大的門控裝置專業設計暨製造公司之一。所查資料未證實其為全球最大的門鎖ODM代工大廠之一，故不保留該排名說法。

來源限制：
- The issuer’s leadership and size descriptions are self-characterizations, not independently verified rankings.
- The issuer’s 2023 description concerns door-control-device design and manufacturing; it cannot be substituted for a ranking of door-lock ODM manufacturers.
- No reliable evidence located provides the period, market boundary, denominator, or comparative data needed to verify the door-lock ODM superlative.

Focused searches located official issuer materials for the founding date, headquarters, and company positioning, plus a secondary repetition of the ODM superlative. Further available evidence did not establish the exact ODM ranking or its market basis, so that atom remains UNKNOWN.
