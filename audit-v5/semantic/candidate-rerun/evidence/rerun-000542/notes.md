## 判定

Overall `CANDIDATE_ACCEPTED`。

建議改寫：As reported in October 2023, MA-tek’s chairperson described TSMC as the company’s biggest client and said the Kumamoto laboratory, opened in September 2023, was established to serve TSMC’s Japanese manufacturing base in Kumamoto. This does not establish that TSMC remains MA-tek’s largest customer today.

來源限制：
- The customer ranking is an attributed 2023 statement; no numerical revenue share or ranking methodology was located, and it should not be carried forward as a current ranking.
- The sources establish the lab and fab in Kumamoto and report the service rationale, but do not provide a measured distance between them.
- Later coverage describes the Kumamoto lab’s role differently: CTEE’s 2025-09-02 report says it mainly served TSMC (https://www.ctee.com.tw/news/20250902701505-430201), while BusinessNext’s 2025-12-31 report says it focused on local firms and served as backup capacity (https://www.bnext.com.tw/article/89653/jasm-2025). This limits conclusions about its current customer mix, not the reported 2023 establishment rationale.

Contemporaneous reporting carrying the chairperson’s direct statement supports the dated historical reading of all atoms. Stopped after focused searches; current customer ranking and exact lab-to-fab distance remain unverified.
