# 重驗筆記 — 敬鵬 / 2355:objective_ranking_or_market_share:e2751dcf4f662431

原始 claim：敬鵬工業 (2355) 是全球第四大[[車用 PCB]] 製造商，全球市占率約 10%。

## 原子主張

1. `UNKNOWN` — 敬鵬工業 (2355) 是全球第四大車用 PCB 製造商。
   期間：Unqualified in the claim. A Taiwan Ministry of Economic Affairs article published in 2022 and a secondary analysis published in 2024 state fourth place, but neither specifies the effective ranking period or ranking metric.
   說明：The dated sources support that this ranking has been reported, but do not establish the period and ranking basis needed to verify the unqualified present-tense claim. Chin Poon's own 2017 investor presentation ranked it first for 2016; that conflicts only for 2016 and does not resolve a later ranking.
   來源：[電動車帶領PCB技術再進化](https://www.moea.gov.tw/MNS/doit/industrytech/IndustryTech.aspx?menu_id=13545&it_id=455)；定位：Paragraph discussing Taiwan automotive PCB makers; states Chin Poon is the world's fourth-largest automotive PCB manufacturer.
   來源：[【PCB】由敬鵬(2355)看車用PCB市場何時復甦](https://uanalyze.com.tw/articles/375404706)；定位：Opening discussion states global fourth place and approximately 10% market share.
   來源：[敬鵬法人說明會 (2017 Investor Conference Presentation, Mandarin)](http://www.chinpoon.com/archive/download/item/20170919%20Chin%20Poon%20Investor%20Conference%20Presentation_Mandarin.pdf)；定位：Slide headed「全球第一大汽車板廠 2016」, market-share table and explanatory notes.
2. `UNKNOWN` — 敬鵬工業全球車用 PCB 市占率約 10%。
   期間：Unqualified in the claim. A secondary analysis published in 2024 states approximately 10%; the issuer's quantified estimates found here are for 2016 only.
   說明：The 2024 secondary source does not disclose the market-size denominator, calculation, or measurement period behind the approximately 10% figure. Chin Poon's 2017 presentation reports 2016 shares of 13.1%, 11.7%, and 10.0% using three different estimated automotive-PCB market denominators; only the last historical estimate is 10.0%, and it cannot verify the unqualified claim.
   來源：[【PCB】由敬鵬(2355)看車用PCB市場何時復甦](https://uanalyze.com.tw/articles/375404706)；定位：Opening discussion states global fourth place and approximately 10% market share.
   來源：[敬鵬法人說明會 (2017 Investor Conference Presentation, Mandarin)](http://www.chinpoon.com/archive/download/item/20170919%20Chin%20Poon%20Investor%20Conference%20Presentation_Mandarin.pdf)；定位：Slide headed「全球第一大汽車板廠 2016」, market-share table and explanatory notes.

## 判定

Overall `UNKNOWN`。

建議改寫：A Taiwan Ministry of Economic Affairs article published on 2022-12-21 described Chin Poon as the world's fourth-largest automotive PCB manufacturer; the article does not specify the ranking's underlying data period or basis.

來源限制：
- The Ministry article is an official dated statement, but it does not identify the ranking's effective period, metric, denominator, or original dataset.
- The 2024 UAnalyze article repeats the fourth-place and approximately 10% assertions without disclosing their underlying data or methodology.
- The issuer's 2016 presentation shows that market-share estimates vary materially by denominator and is too old to establish the unqualified claim.

Eight focused research turns were used. The claim remains unresolved because available current-context assertions lack a disclosed measurement period and ranking or market-share basis; older issuer data cannot be substituted for the unqualified claim.
