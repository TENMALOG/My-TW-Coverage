## 判定

Overall `PARTIAL`。

建議改寫：Taiwan Paiho’s business origin dates to 1979, while the current legal company lists its establishment date as January 14, 1985. Its headquarters are in Hemei Township, Changhua County, Taiwan. It manufactures hook-and-loop fasteners and footwear accessories; a global ‘one of the largest’ ranking is not independently verified by the evidence reviewed.

來源限制：
- The issuer’s own materials distinguish the business’s 1979 origin from the current legal company’s 1985 establishment date.
- The global-size statements found are promotional or secondary claims without a sufficiently defined market, period, comparison set, or denominator.
- Evidence about supplying major sports brands or being a large supplier to those brands does not establish global market rank.

Focused searches confirmed the founding-origin, headquarters, and product-scope atoms. Searches for ranking evidence surfaced company promotional statements and secondary reporting but no independently verifiable global ranking with a defined period and denominator; the ranking atoms remain UNKNOWN.
