## Findings

The official issuer pages substantiate that 燁鋒 makes and supplies aerospace-use aluminum materials in plate, tube, bar, and hot-rolled wire-rod forms. Its Chinese page's structured metadata says it is the only Asian mill with full-process capability for the listed forms. The same issuer's English homepage, however, describes the full-process capability as held by “one of few” mills in Taiwan. Neither page supplies a comparative methodology or a region-wide list of competitors.

The AIDC article directly verifies that 燁鋒 joined the six-company A-Team 4.0 aerospace raw-material supplier alliance on 2016-09-02. It does not verify a market-wide uniqueness claim.

The comparative qualifier therefore remains `UNKNOWN` as a factual assertion. The original compound claim remains `PARTIAL`; the candidate rewrite removes the unverified “亞洲唯一” qualifier and binds the alliance statement to 2016.
