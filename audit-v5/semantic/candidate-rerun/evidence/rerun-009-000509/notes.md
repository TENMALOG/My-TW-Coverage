## 判定

Overall `PARTIAL`。

建議改寫：Alconix engages in non-ferrous scrap recycling and reports a domestic network of scrap dealers. In 2025 it stated an aim to become Japan's largest non-ferrous scrap collector by volume; the sources reviewed do not establish that it had achieved that ranking or that it operated a global scrap procurement network.

來源限制：
- The ranking and network evidence is primarily issuer-reported; no independent market-wide ranking with a defined market, metric, denominator, and period was located.
- The issuer's stated goal concerns non-ferrous scrap collection volume, which is narrower than the claim's undefined description of the largest metal recycler.
- The article's discussion of overseas sourcing is prospective and does not establish a current global procurement network.

Focused searches and review of official Alconix business, group, and recycling disclosures established recycling activity and a domestic supplier network, but did not resolve the broad ranking or global-network assertions. Those atoms remain UNKNOWN pending comparable independent ranking data and evidence defining the current geographic scope of scrap procurement.
