- Customer-name evidence is partly from secondary reporting. Rexon's partner list corroborates brand association but does not, by itself, prove customer status or that a customer is major.
- No market-share or industry-ranking claim was assessed.

Focused research did not retrieve period-specific official product-category revenue figures establishing the claimed revenue ranking. Customer relationships were partially corroborated; the unresolved ranking remains UNKNOWN.
