## 判定

Overall `PARTIAL`。

建議改寫：A company-briefing summary updated June 5, 2025 lists Williams-Sonoma, Costco, and NITORI among 峰源-KY's direct-sales customers in the 2024 business context. A separate report published April 25, 2022 stated that Costco private-label sofas and other furniture represented 11% of company revenue, but did not identify the fiscal period for that percentage.

來源限制：
- The 2024 official annual report describes the company's customers generically in the business narrative; the named relationships and Costco's 11% figure here rely on secondary reporting.
- No located evidence verifies that Costco's revenue share remained above 10% after the 2022 report. The 2022 percentage's underlying fiscal period is unspecified.

Focused searches and review of issuer investor materials and contemporaneous financial reporting found dated support for the customer relationships and a historical Costco share, but not a fiscal-period-specific or later-period Costco percentage. The unqualified percentage atom remains UNKNOWN.
