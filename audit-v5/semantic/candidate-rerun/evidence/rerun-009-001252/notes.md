## 判定

Overall `PARTIAL`。

建議改寫：MoneyDJ reported in January 2018 that chemical-grade and clean-grade products each represented about half of YHMCO revenue at that time, and that Japan was a principal export market for chemical-grade products used in petrochemical-related industries. Available evidence does not establish the exact underlying accounting period or that Japanese petrochemical companies specifically received most exports.

來源限制：
- The approximately 50% revenue share is historical secondary reporting; the exact accounting period and consolidated-versus-parent revenue basis are not stated.
- Issuer disclosures support Japan as a major market but also name the United States; no quantified Japan share of chemical-grade exports was located.
- Evidence about petrochemical applications and Japan as an export destination does not by itself establish that exports were principally to Japanese petrochemical buyers.

Focused searches and review of issuer reports and company product information found support for the historical revenue-share and Japan-market components, but no evidence resolving the Japan-specific customer-industry linkage or establishing the unqualified claim for a defined current period.
