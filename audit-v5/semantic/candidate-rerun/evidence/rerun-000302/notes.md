## 判定

Overall `PARTIAL`。

建議改寫：A May 2015 secondary report stated that 茂林 captured nearly 80% of Apple's illuminated-keyboard orders at that time; the available evidence does not establish a MacBook-specific or continuing largest-supplier ranking.

來源限制：
- The Apple-specific evidence is secondary reporting attributed to 工商時報 and hosted by IEK, not an issuer or regulator disclosure.
- The nearly 80% figure is a reported share of Apple's illuminated-keyboard orders; the report does not define a broader market denominator or identify MacBook models.
- The issuer's 2014 annual report independently describes 茂林 as a major global supplier of illuminated-keyboard light-guide plates, but does not name Apple or quantify its Apple-specific share.

Stopped after seven focused research turns, within the eight-turn limit. Historical evidence supports an Apple illuminated-keyboard supply relationship and near-80% order share as reported in 2015; MacBook-specific and undated/continuing ranking scope remain unresolved.
