# 重驗筆記 — 欣泰 / 8917:material_named_relationship:73633ab28e6947bb

原始 claim：[[台灣中油]]（CPC，天然氣獨家供應來源）

## 原子主張

1. `SUPPORTED` — As of 欣泰’s FY2024 annual-report disclosure, 台灣中油 (CPC) was 欣泰’s exclusive natural-gas source.
   期間：FY2024 disclosure; the supplier table also reports data through Q1 2025.
   說明：The issuer directly states that its natural-gas source is exclusively 台灣中油. This supports the relationship for the report’s disclosure period, but does not independently establish that it remained unchanged through October 7, 2026. The exclusive-source wording concerns natural-gas sourcing; the report’s aggregate-purchase percentages use a broader denominator and should not be treated as a natural-gas-only percentage. The relationship is qualitative; no unit or quantified denominator is given.
   來源：[欣泰石油氣股份有限公司 113年度年報](https://www.htgas.com.tw/webdoc/060000000-%E6%8A%95%E8%B3%87%E4%BA%BA%E5%B0%88%E5%8D%80/060100000-%E6%8A%95%E8%B3%87%E4%BA%BA%E5%B0%88%E5%8D%80/060103000-%E8%82%A1%E6%9D%B1%E6%9C%83%E8%B3%87%E8%A8%8A/060103011-%E8%82%A1%E6%9D%B1%E6%9C%83%E8%B3%87%E8%A8%8A/zh/114/%E6%AC%A3%E6%B3%B0%E7%9F%B3%E6%B2%B9113%E5%B9%B4%E8%82%A1%E6%9D%B1%E6%9C%83%E5%B9%B4%E5%A0%B1.pdf)；定位：Page 55, sections (三)主要原料之供應狀況 and (四)最近二年度主要供應商資料.

## 判定

Overall `PARTIAL`。

建議改寫：依欣泰民國113年度年報所載，截至該揭露期間，台灣中油為欣泰天然氣的獨家氣源供應商。

來源限制：
- A link to 欣泰’s 2025 (ROC year 114) annual report was located, but the underlying PDF timed out when opened; its contents could not be checked for a newer confirmation.
- The annual report’s aggregate-purchase table is not the denominator for the narrower claim about the source of natural gas.

The issuer’s FY2024 report directly supports the relationship for its disclosure period. Current status beyond that period remains unresolved because the later annual-report PDF could not be retrieved.
