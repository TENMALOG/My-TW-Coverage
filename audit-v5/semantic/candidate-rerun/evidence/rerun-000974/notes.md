## 判定

Overall `PARTIAL`。

建議改寫：A secondary report published March 31, 2025, states that 三希科技 accounted for about 85% of 大眾控's FY2024 revenue. Issuer-affiliated pages describe 三希科技 as an EMS manufacturer with optical-module, industrial-control, and automotive-electronics operations, and report longstanding cooperation with top domestic and international optical-module companies. The customer relationship is supported; the claim that these firms constitute its main customer base, and the continued validity of the 85% share, are not established.

來源限制：
- The approximately 85% figure is from a secondary analysis explicitly referring to FY2024; it was not independently reconciled to an audited subsidiary-revenue calculation.
- The original claim gives no period for the 85% figure. Available evidence supports a historical FY2024 figure only.
- The customer sources establish relationships with leading optical-module firms, but do not quantify customer concentration or prove those firms are the main customer group by revenue or volume.
- The issuer-affiliated web pages have no stated publication or effective date.

Focused review of issuer-affiliated company pages and contemporaneous secondary reporting found support for the FY2024 revenue-share statement, EMS/business areas, and customer relationships. The current revenue share and the customer-base ranking remain unresolved; research stopped within the specified search-turn limit.
