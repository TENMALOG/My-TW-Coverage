## 判定

Overall `PARTIAL`。

建議改寫：Historical evidence supports reports of approximately 530,000 ping of nationwide land and approximately 35,800 ping in the Nangang Trade and Economic Park, with the latter figure counting a 50-year superficies-lease portion; current holdings and the Taipei City-wide largest-landowner ranking remain unverified. Taiwan Fertilizer's 2015 disclosures support NT$15 billion in first-stage C3 superficies consideration and C2 project investment exceeding NT$10 billion.

來源限制：
- The nationwide and Nangang area figures are from secondary reporting, and the reports do not establish current holdings.
- The 35,800-ping figure includes an area counted despite a 50-year superficies lease; it should not be represented as unqualified current outright ownership.
- Evidence found for a largest-landowner ranking refers to Nangang District, not Taipei City.
- The issuer describes C3's NT$15 billion as first-stage consideration; the same article describes a second stage and NT$30 billion across both stages.
- C2 investment evidence is a 2015 issuer-reported project estimate, not verification of final or current total cost.
