## 判定

Overall `PARTIAL`。

建議改寫：永裕為塑膠包材製造商，產品包括塑膠瓶、軟管及射出成品，並提供產品與模具設計開發及相關製造加工服務。

來源限制：
- The issuer’s website is primary evidence for its own products and described capabilities, but not independent evidence of market leadership.
- The secondary MoneyDJ ranking statement concerns domestic daily-chemical containers, not precisely the claim’s cosmetics-and-cleaning-products container market; it supplies no denominator or methodology.

Focused searches and source checks found support for the product and capability wording but no reliable evidence resolving the exact Taiwan-largest ranking, its period, or market denominator.
