# 重驗筆記 — 環球晶 / 6488:objective_ranking_or_market_share:d5518e9a98f8f76b

原始 claim：[[環球晶]]圓股份有限公司 (6488，GlobalWafers) 隸屬於[[中美晶]]集團，是全球前三大、台灣第一大的[[矽晶圓]]供應商。

## 原子主張

1. `SUPPORTED` — GlobalWafers Co., Ltd. (6488) is part of the Sino-American Silicon Products (SAS) group.
   期間：Current group relationship; GlobalWafers was spun off from SAS in 2011.
   說明：SAS’s current company profile describes the 2011 semiconductor-business separation into GlobalWafers and identifies GlobalWafers as part of the group’s semiconductor business. This supports group affiliation, not a claim that SAS currently owns 100% of GlobalWafers.
   來源：[公司簡介 | 中美矽晶製品股份有限公司](https://www.saswafer.com/profile-tw/)；定位：「以策略分工與平台治理，放大集團綜效」 and 「在半導體領域，集團透過環球晶圓股份有限公司」
2. `SUPPORTED` — GlobalWafers is one of the world’s top three silicon wafer suppliers.
   期間：Company profile dated June 2025; also stated in an undated issuer profile.
   說明：GlobalWafers states it is the world’s third-largest silicon wafer supplier. The relevant market is silicon wafer manufacturing/supply; the issuer materials describe products spanning 3-inch to 12-inch wafers. The ranking is ordinal, so no unit applies. The issuer does not disclose the ranking’s measurement basis (such as revenue, shipment area, or capacity) or a quantified peer-set denominator.
   來源：[GlobalWafers Company Profile (June 2025)](https://www.sas-globalwafers.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/GWC-Company-Profile-EN202506.pdf)；定位：“Strengths”: “The 3rd largest silicon wafer supplier in the world”; “Complete Product Offering”: 3-inch to 12-inch wafers
   來源：[GlobalWafers Reports Full Year 2025 Results](https://www.taipeitimes.com/News/biz/archives/2025/05/07/2003836433)；定位：Article lead describes GlobalWafers as “the world’s third-largest silicon wafer supplier.”
3. `SUPPORTED` — GlobalWafers is Taiwan’s largest silicon wafer supplier.
   期間：Undated issuer profile; independently described as current in January 2026.
   說明：The issuer identifies itself as Taiwan’s largest wafer manufacturer and specifies 3-inch to 12-inch silicon wafer manufacturing. The relevant denominator is Taiwan-based suppliers/manufacturers in that silicon-wafer scope; the issuer does not publish a ranking metric, quantified denominator, or units for the ordinal rank.
   來源：[Group Profile - GlobalWafers Co., Ltd.](https://www.sas-globalwafers.com/en/group-profile/)；定位：“Company Profile”: “GlobalWafers Co., Ltd. is Taiwan’s largest, world’s third-largest … wafer manufacturer” specializing in 3-inch to 12-inch silicon wafers
   來源：[環球晶股價1月大漲25%，站上500元大關！為何2026年展望可能比2025年更優？晶圓女王親解三大動能](https://www.businesstoday.com.tw/article/category/183015/post/202601210070)；定位：Article lead calls GlobalWafers “台灣最大、全球第三大矽晶圓供應商”

## 判定

Overall `CANDIDATE_ACCEPTED`。

建議改寫：環球晶圓股份有限公司（6488，GlobalWafers）隸屬於中美晶集團，是全球前三大、台灣第一大的矽晶圓供應商。

來源限制：
- The issuer’s ranking statements and the secondary reports do not state whether rank is based on revenue, shipment area, capacity, or another measure.
- The issuer’s Taiwan/world ranking page is undated; the June 2025 company profile gives a dated basis for the global top-three claim, and a January 2026 secondary report corroborates the Taiwan and global rankings.

Focused research found direct issuer support for group affiliation and both rankings, with secondary corroboration. No further research was needed; ranking measurement methodology remains undisclosed.
