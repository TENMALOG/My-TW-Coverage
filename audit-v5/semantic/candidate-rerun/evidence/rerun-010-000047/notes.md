   來源：[Autoliv, Inc. Form 10-K for fiscal year ended December 31, 2024](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1034670/000095017025023852/alv-20241231.htm)；定位：Item 1, Business; market position discussion
   來源：[Future vehicle interiors: ZF LIFETEC presents next generation airbags and steering wheels](https://press.zf.com/press/en/releases/release_70280.html)；定位：Opening paragraphs, global passive-safety market share statement

## 判定

Overall `PARTIAL`。

建議改寫：Frost & Sullivan data reproduced in Joyson Electronics’ draft prospectus reports that the three largest suppliers held about 91.9% of the 2024 global automotive passive-safety market by revenue. That market includes airbags, seatbelts, and steering wheels; the prospectus anonymizes two suppliers, so this does not verify a 90%+ combined share for Autoliv, ZF, and Joyson in the narrower airbag/seatbelt-component market.

來源限制：
- The closest quantified evidence is an issuer-hosted draft prospectus using commissioned Frost & Sullivan estimates, not an official regulator market census.
- The prospectus’s global passive-safety denominator includes steering wheels as well as airbags and seatbelts; its unnamed top-three figure cannot be directly assigned to the exact named companies from that table.
- Individual supplier market-share figures are not interchangeable across differing market definitions or denominators.

Focused searches and official issuer sources established the suppliers’ product categories and found a nearby 2024 aggregate figure, but not evidence resolving both the named-company identity and the narrower market denominator. The remaining share atom is therefore UNKNOWN.
