# 重驗筆記 — 承啟 / 2425:identity_changing_event:55f86d211d68f828

原始 claim：公司原名承啟大眾科技 (Walton Chaintech)，2013 年更名為承啟科技。

## 原子主張

1. `CONTRADICTED` — Before the 2013 change, the company’s registered Chinese name was 承啟大眾科技.
   期間：Before January 16, 2013
   說明：The Ministry of Economic Affairs’ company registry lists the prior registered name as 華東承啟科技股份有限公司, not 承啟大眾科技. The claim’s English name is addressed separately.
   來源：[承啟科技股份有限公司 — 經濟部商工登記公示資料查詢](https://findbiz.nat.gov.tw/fts/company/22332192?fhl=en)；定位：Company name-change history; entry dated 2013/01/16
2. `SUPPORTED` — Walton Chaintech Corporation was a former English name associated with the company.
   期間：Before the 2013 name change
   說明：A securities filing identifies WALTON CHAINTECH CORPORATION and gives its Chinese name as 華東承啟科技股份有限公司.
   來源：[Shenzhen Stock Exchange filing mentioning WALTON CHAINTECH CORPORATION](https://disc.static.szse.cn/disc/disk01/finalpage/2015-05-25/7f6c9de7-068e-457e-8dc2-69e2be9e963e.PDF)；定位：Filing text: “WALTON CHAINTECH CORPORATION” and “中文名称为华东承启科技股份有限公司”
3. `SUPPORTED` — The company changed its registered Chinese name to 承啟科技股份有限公司 in 2013.
   期間：2013
   說明：The official registry records 承啟科技股份有限公司 from January 16, 2013. A reproduced company announcement also states the name change was approved by the Ministry on that date.
   來源：[承啟科技股份有限公司 — 經濟部商工登記公示資料查詢](https://findbiz.nat.gov.tw/fts/company/22332192?fhl=en)；定位：Company name-change history; entry dated 2013/01/16
   來源：[承啟：公告本公司名稱由「華東承啟科技股份有限公司」更名為「承啟科技股份有限公司」](https://news.cnyes.com/news/id/1757387)；定位：Announcement text, items 1 and 5

## 判定

Overall `PARTIAL`。

建議改寫：公司原名為華東承啟科技股份有限公司，英文名為 Walton Chaintech Corporation；2013 年更名為承啟科技股份有限公司。

來源限制：
- The official registry supports the legal Chinese names and the dated transition, but does not establish the exact phrase 承啟大眾科技 as a registered name.
- The Walton Chaintech Corporation name and its Chinese-name pairing are supported by a later securities filing describing the company’s historical identity.

Focused searches found official registry evidence for the 2013 Chinese-name change and filing evidence for the prior English name; the asserted prior Chinese name conflicts with the registry record.
