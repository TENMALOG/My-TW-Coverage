## 判定

Overall `PARTIAL`。

建議改寫：Issuer materials support that AI applications represented 15.49% of application revenue in 2024 H1, up from 5.14% in 2023, and that a molded TLVR product had an initial delivery to an unnamed brand customer by 2026-05-28. They do not establish that this molded TLVR was supplied for both NVIDIA and AMD AI-server platforms or GPU mainboards; the issuer separately reported NVIDIA-platform low-height TLVR still in testing and Intel/AMD server-CPU TLVR at production/introduction stages.

來源限制：
- The 2024 15.49% figure is an AI-only application mix for 2024 H1; later issuer disclosures use combined AI/server or AI/automotive/server categories, which are not interchangeable.
- The 2026 molded-TLVR initial delivery disclosure does not name the customer, platform, or product SKU.
- GPU-board development cooperation with NVIDIA and AMD is described separately from TLVR product stages; the reviewed issuer materials do not connect that cooperation to the delivered molded TLVR.

Stopped after reviewing issuer presentations spanning 2024-09 through 2026-05. These establish the historical AI-share figure and current product-stage distinctions, but do not disclose the named-platform mapping needed to resolve the remaining relationship atoms.
