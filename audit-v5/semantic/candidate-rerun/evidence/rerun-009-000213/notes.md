## 判定

Overall `PARTIAL`。

建議改寫：Tatung reported in October 2025 that it supplied smart meters to power companies in Thailand and Malaysia. Company disclosures confirm Taipower contracts, but the reviewed evidence does not establish Taipower as Tatung's largest domestic customer or confirm Middle East power companies as existing customers.

來源限制：
- The claim gives no reference period, business segment, or metric for “largest domestic customer”; contract awards and a report that domestic Power Business Group sales mostly came from Taipower do not prove that ranking.
- The Southeast Asia evidence is limited to smart meters and named utility markets in Thailand and Malaysia.
- The Middle East disclosures describe business development and pursuit of orders, not identified completed sales to power companies.

Stopped after focused research found direct issuer support for Southeast Asian utility supply but no evidence resolving the Taipower ranking or actual Middle East power-company customer relationship; those atoms remain UNKNOWN.
