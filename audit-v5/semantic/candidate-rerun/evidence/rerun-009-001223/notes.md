## 判定

Overall `CANDIDATE_ACCEPTED`。

建議改寫：IKKA 集團的汽車零組件供應模式為 Tier 2 → 日系 Tier 1 → 終端車廠。公司資料列出住友電裝／Sumitomo Electric 及 AISIN，並將 Toyota、Nissan、Honda 列為終端車廠或合作開發對象；公開資料未逐一對應各 Tier 1 與各車廠。

來源限制：
- Evidence is primarily issuer disclosure: a 2025 investor presentation and the 2025 annual report published 2026-05-15.
- The investor presentation’s major-customer revenue chart does not identify its reference period; no customer percentages are relied on as proof of the qualitative claim.
- The issuer shows the supply-chain relationship at a group level, not a supplier-by-supplier mapping to Toyota, Nissan, or Honda.
- Issuer materials use varying Sumitomo names, including Sumitomo Electric, Sumitomo Electric Wiring Systems, and 住友電裝; exact legal-entity equivalence is not established here.

Official issuer disclosures support the full qualitative claim. Further research was unnecessary for candidate-level verification; the specific Tier 1-to-OEM pairings remain unstated and are not asserted.
