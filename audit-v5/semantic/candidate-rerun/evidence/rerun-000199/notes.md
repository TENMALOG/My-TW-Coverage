## 判定

Overall `CANDIDATE_ACCEPTED`。

建議改寫：CMoney research articles published in May 2022 and August 2023 attributed approximately 56% of Wiwynn revenue to Meta and described Meta as a larger customer than Microsoft. The articles do not specify the underlying measurement period or revenue basis; this does not establish the undated or current claim.

來源限制：
- The named-customer percentages found are secondary-source claims, not confirmed by a named customer-specific revenue breakdown in the official disclosures located during this review.
- Wiwynn's official Q3 2022 financial report discusses concentration of accounts receivable among specific customers, which is a receivables measure and cannot substitute for customer revenue share: https://wiwynn.com/hubfs/Investors/Financial_Report/Wiwynn_2022Q3_Financial.pdf
- A July 31, 2025 Cnyes expert-opinion article says Meta was Wiwynn's second-largest customer while also saying its share exceeded 50%; that conflicts with the ranking in this claim if both statements use the same denominator, and the article does not resolve the inconsistency: https://news.cnyes.com/news/id/6084299
- The repeated approximately 56% figure in the CMoney articles is not accompanied by a clearly identified fiscal period or denominator methodology.

Focused searches located historical secondary reports supporting the attributed ranking and approximate share, but not an issuer-confirmed named-customer revenue breakdown. The claim has no reference period, and the retrieved sources do not establish whether it is intended as a current assertion.
