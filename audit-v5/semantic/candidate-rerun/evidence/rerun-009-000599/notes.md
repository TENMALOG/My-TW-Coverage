## 判定

Overall `PARTIAL`。

建議改寫：欣銓提供車用半導體測試服務，二手報導列出 TI、NXP、STMicro、Infineon、Renesas 等國際 IDM 為重要客戶；目前未核實其車用 MCU 測試市場市占率超過八成，也未找到正式指定測試合作夥伴資格的官方確認。

來源限制：
- The located “over 80%” figure refers to the combined automotive MCU market share of five customer IDMs, not 欣銓’s share of automotive MCU testing services.
- The reports identifying automotive IDM customers are secondary sources. Customer relationships do not by themselves establish a testing-market share or formal designation.
- No official issuer or regulator disclosure located in this research establishes the claimed testing-market denominator, measurement period, or greater-than-80% figure.

Focused searches and source checks located relevant customer and market-share context, but no official evidence for the claimed testing-services market share or formal designation. Further inference from the customers’ MCU market share would substitute a different denominator.
