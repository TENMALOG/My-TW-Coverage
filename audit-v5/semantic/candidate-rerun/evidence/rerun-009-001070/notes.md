## 判定

Overall `UNKNOWN`。

建議改寫：一份標示2002/09資料的次級來源將Toshiba、HPQ、Dell列為彩晶主要客戶；該資料不能證明這些關係延續至今，也未核實Acer或Sony。

來源限制：
- The 2025 annual report is an issuer report reproduced in English by a third-party filing site; it says the Chinese report governs interpretation.
- The 2025 report's customer disclosure uses Company A/B/C and does not identify the brands. This is a disclosure limitation, not evidence that the named firms were not customers.
- The historical MoneyDJ customer list is dated to data marked 2002/09 and cannot substantiate an unqualified or current claim.

Focused searches found current issuer-report evidence with anonymized customer names and limited historical secondary evidence for HPQ, Dell, and Toshiba. No reliable evidence resolved the unspecified-period claims for all five brands; adjacent brand lists and unattributed summaries were not treated as proof.
