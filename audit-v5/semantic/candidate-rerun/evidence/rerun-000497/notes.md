- Issuer material supports its storage-media target business and global leadership, but does not provide the 35% figure in the material located.
- The 35% figure is supported only by secondary reporting citing unnamed market sources; its denominator basis and effective measurement period are undisclosed.

Focused searches covered issuer investor materials, company profiles, and financial-news reporting. No official disclosure located resolved the 35% share estimate's measurement period or denominator basis.
