## 判定

Overall `PARTIAL`。

建議改寫：A secondary report described Cummins as 光隆精密-KY’s largest customer and said the named North American truck makers sourced engines from Cummins. The available evidence does not establish that parts supplied by 光隆精密 were incorporated into Cummins engines delivered to PACCAR, Daimler Truck, Volvo Trucks, or Traton.

來源限制：
- The largest-customer ranking is supported by secondary reporting; the later company-attributed wording is specifically “largest commercial-vehicle customer,” and neither source states the ranking’s measurement period or revenue denominator.
- Cummins’ official model page documents engine availability, not confirmed purchases by each OEM or the inclusion of components supplied by 光隆精密.
- The 2024 secondary report describes the OEMs as customers while also discussing their Cummins engine sourcing; it does not trace the claimed indirect parts-supply path.

Focused searches found support for the Cummins customer ranking and adjacent evidence of Cummins engine availability in some named OEM models, but no reliable source tracing 光隆精密-supplied parts through Cummins to any of the four OEMs. Those linkage atoms remain UNKNOWN.
