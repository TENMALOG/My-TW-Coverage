## 判定

Overall `PARTIAL`。

建議改寫：慶騰的鐵粉供應商名單包含瑞典 Höganäs（另有其他供應商）。Höganäs官方稱其為全球領先鐵粉製造商；「全球最大」尚無明確排名口徑及比較數據支持。

來源限制：
- The supplier evidence is secondary and does not state the effective period or purchase volumes.
- The latest issuer annual report reviewed did not identify Höganäs by name as a supplier; that omission is not evidence against the supplier relationship.
- Höganäs’s self-description as “world-leading” does not establish that it is the largest.

Focused research found secondary support for Höganäs being one of multiple iron-powder suppliers and official evidence for its Swedish origin. No period-specific primary supplier disclosure or evidence establishing the claimed global-largest ranking and its measurement basis was found; the ranking atom remains UNKNOWN.
