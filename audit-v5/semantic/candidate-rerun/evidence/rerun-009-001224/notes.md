## 判定

Overall `PARTIAL`。

建議改寫：晶呈科技2025年年報列示其特殊氣體用途包括半導體蝕刻、層膜及曝光準分子雷射；2022年《商業周刊》報導其氣瓶供應台積電與聯電。現有證據未確認Micron為客戶、台積電按何種口徑屬「主要客戶」，或產品特定用於CVD。

來源限制：
- Named customer relationships for TSMC and UMC are supported by secondary reporting from 2022, not by a current named-customer issuer disclosure.
- The issuer's FY2025 annual report reports sales amounts and percentages for anonymized customer codes; it does not map those codes to TSMC, UMC, or Micron.
- The annual report distinguishes etching, layer-film, and exposure excimer-laser applications. It does not specifically name CVD.

Focused searches and review of the issuer’s FY2025 annual report and relevant secondary reporting did not resolve Micron’s customer status, TSMC’s asserted major-customer scope or denominator, or CVD-specific use. Unresolved atoms remain UNKNOWN rather than inferred.
