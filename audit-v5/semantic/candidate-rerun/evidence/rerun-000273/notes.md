## 判定

Overall `PARTIAL`。

建議改寫：Reporting dated 2026-10-06 identifies Largan as a supplier of high-end handset camera lenses to Apple, Samsung, and Xiaomi. A 2024 report describes 2021 long-term supply agreements covering selected high-end lens orders with OPPO and vivo. Apple's status as Largan's largest customer remains unverified on a specified metric and period.

來源限制：
- The supplied claim is a fragment with no explicit predicate or time scope. The names after Apple are interpreted as an alleged customer roster.
- The 2026 customer-supply report is secondary reporting and does not quantify each customer's revenue, shipments, or share of Largan's business.
- The OPPO and vivo evidence is historical and concerns selected high-end lens orders; it does not establish a current relationship.
- The secondary claim that Apple is the largest customer lacks a stated ranking basis, denominator, and measurement period. The issuer filing section located reports sales by region, not named customers.

Focused searches found contemporaneous reporting for Apple, Samsung, and Xiaomi and historical agreement reporting for OPPO and vivo. The largest-customer ranking remains unresolved because its metric, denominator, and period are not established by the located sources.
