# 重驗筆記 — 中美實 / 4702:profitability_assertion:6f5e05252bf8c5fc

原始 claim：業務轉向汽車貿易及醫療器材代理 (代理[[Mercedes-Benz]]等品牌中古車)，投資收益為主要獲利來源。

## 原子主張

1. `SUPPORTED` — 中美實的業務曾轉向或包含汽車貿易。
   期間：2023–2024; the company says it suspended the related business starting in 2025.
   說明：Issuer disclosures identify automotive trade as a business and describe the company as having shifted to a trading model. The issuer also says automotive trade slowed from 2024 and was suspended from 2025, so this supports a historical—not current—description. Scope: issuer/group business; no market denominator or unit applies.
   來源：[中美聯合實業股份有限公司及子公司 2024 年永續報告書](https://aicgroup.com.tw/upload/files/202510220727324ph4d.pdf)；定位：Page 12, lines 495–498
   來源：[中美聯合實業股份有限公司 2024 年報](https://www.aicgroup.com.tw/upload/files/2024.pdf)；定位：pp. 47–49, lines 2152–2155 and 2220–2238
2. `UNKNOWN` — 醫療器材代理是中美實已實際營運的業務。
   期間：Undated company webpage presents it as a future business area; actual operating period not established.
   說明：The issuer webpage lists medical-device agency among businesses it intended to introduce or expand into. That prospective wording does not verify that the company actually operated the agency business or generated revenue from it. No market denominator or units are disclosed.
   來源：[新事業 | 中美聯合實業股份有限公司](https://www.aicgroup.com.tw/about/3)；定位：Lines 27–37
   來源：[中美聯合實業股份有限公司 2026 年法人說明會](https://www.aicgroup.com.tw/upload/files/20260831062641d1sdq.pdf)；定位：Page 2, lines 21–24; page 4, lines 37–51
3. `UNKNOWN` — 中美實代理 Mercedes-Benz 等品牌中古車。
   期間：Claim period unspecified; issuer disclosure describes the 2024 business but does not identify Mercedes-Benz or used vehicles.
   說明：The issuer's 2024 sustainability report describes obtaining new cars through U.S. agents and selling them directly to end customers, principally in China. It does not verify Mercedes-Benz, used-car sales, or the broader brand-specific agency claim. This evidence is insufficient to establish the claim across an unspecified period. Units and market denominator are not provided.
   來源：[中美聯合實業股份有限公司及子公司 2024 年永續報告書](https://aicgroup.com.tw/upload/files/202510220727324ph4d.pdf)；定位：Page 12, lines 495–498
4. `UNKNOWN` — 投資收益為中美實主要獲利來源。
   期間：Issuer presentation reports FY2025 and 2026 Q1–Q2; claim period and profit measure are otherwise unspecified.
   說明：The issuer says investing in listed stocks is intended to increase earnings. Its presentation reports consolidated non-operating income and expenses of NT$100.874 million in FY2025 and NT$81.969 million in 2026 H1, alongside operating losses of NT$35.652 million and NT$20.530 million, respectively. However, it does not disaggregate those non-operating results sufficiently to establish that investment income itself was the primary profit source. The denominator and meaning of “主要” are unspecified.
   來源：[中美聯合實業股份有限公司 2026 年法人說明會](https://www.aicgroup.com.tw/upload/files/20260831062641d1sdq.pdf)；定位：Page 2, lines 21–24; page 4, lines 37–51

## 判定

Overall `PARTIAL`。

建議改寫：中美實歷史上曾經營汽車貿易，並表示自2025年起暫停相關業務；醫療器材代理是否已實際營運、是否代理 Mercedes-Benz 等品牌中古車，以及投資收益是否為主要獲利來源，均未由所查證據完整確認。

來源限制：
- 汽車貿易的歷史證據不能外推為2025年以後仍在經營；公司披露自2025年起暫停相關業務。
- 公司網頁列出醫療器材代理屬未來拓展方向，不等於已實際提供代理服務。
- 汽車業務的官方描述是新車交易，未提供 Mercedes-Benz 品牌或中古車代理的直接證據。
- 官方簡報將非營業收入及支出合併列示，不能直接等同投資收益。

已查閱發行人年報、永續報告、公司業務頁及2026年8月31日法說會資料；對未提供實際營運證據、品牌／中古車細節或非營業損益構成的原子主張，現有資料不足，故保留 UNKNOWN。
