## 判定

Overall `PARTIAL`。

建議改寫：東鹼稱硫酸鉀98%外銷，並透過各地經銷商及代理商拓展全球市場；FY2025個體財報列有印度、巴基斯坦、日本及澳洲的化工產品收入，但未證實這些國家均有硫酸鉀經銷商。

來源限制：
- The 98% figure is for potassium sulfate, not all company exports; its precise denominator and unit are not disclosed in the cited presentation.
- FY2025 country revenue figures are for chemical products at the parent-company level, not specifically potassium sulfate and not evidence of distributor relationships.
- The issuer confirms a worldwide distributor/agent approach but does not publicly identify distributors by the four named countries in the sources reviewed.

Reviewed issuer sales-channel information, its 2025 investor presentation, and FY2025 audited financial statements. The export percentage and global channel approach are supported, but official disclosures located do not establish named distributor relationships in each country; those atoms remain UNKNOWN.
