# 重驗筆記 — 鴻海 / 2317:objective_ranking_or_market_share:862167a3881f91f4

原始 claim：[[鴻海]]精密 (2317) 是全球最大的電子代工服務 ([[EMS]]) 廠商，2023 年營收 6.16 兆新台幣。

## 原子主張

1. `SUPPORTED` — 鴻海精密 (2317) 是全球最大的電子代工服務 (EMS) 廠商。
   期間：2023 sales ranking; the claim itself does not specify a year
   說明：New Venture Research’s MMI Top 50 list places HonHai Precision (Foxconn) at No. 1. The ranking is for global EMS providers and is based on provider sales; MMI says its survey covers over 100 large EMS companies worldwide. This supports the claim as the leading company in that global EMS ranking, with the ranking’s survey scope noted as a caveat.
   來源：[Top 50 EMS Companies for 2024 - New Venture Research](https://newventureresearch.com/the-mmi-top-50-for-2024/)；定位：Top 50 table, No. 1; closing paragraph identifying the list as the 2023 Top 50 appearing in the March 2024 MMI edition
   來源：[Top 50 EMS Companies - 2024 (MMI press release)](https://www.circuitnet.com/news/uploads/4/Top_50_EMS_Companies_-_2024.pdf)；定位：Opening paragraphs and paragraph beginning “In addition to ranking providers by 2023 sales”
2. `SUPPORTED` — 鴻海精密 2023 年營收為 6.16 兆新台幣。
   期間：FY2023
   說明：The issuer reported full-year revenue of NT$6.162 trillion, which rounds to NT$6.16 trillion. Scope is Hon Hai’s consolidated group revenue; unit is New Taiwan dollars (NT$), expressed in trillions.
   來源：[Hon Hai Announces FY2023 & 4Q23 Financial Results - Hon Hai Technology Group](https://www.foxconn.com/en-us/press-center/press-releases/latest-news/1299)；定位：Full-year 2023 financial results, revenue paragraph

## 判定

Overall `CANDIDATE_ACCEPTED`。

建議改寫：鴻海精密 (2317) 是全球最大的電子代工服務 (EMS) 廠商，2023 年營收 6.16 兆新台幣。

來源限制：
- The ranking is a specialized industry survey rather than a regulator or issuer filing. It ranks the MMI Top 50, compiled from a survey of over 100 large EMS providers; the MMI release estimates the Top 50 usually represents approximately 85–90% of industry revenue.
- The New Venture Research list page’s heading refers to a 2024 list, while its closing text identifies the list as the 2023 ranking. The contemporaneous MMI release specifies that the ranking is based on 2023 sales. The 2023 revenue figure is directly supported by the issuer.

The ranking and revenue atoms are supported by a published MMI ranking/list and an official issuer financial-results disclosure; no further research was needed.
