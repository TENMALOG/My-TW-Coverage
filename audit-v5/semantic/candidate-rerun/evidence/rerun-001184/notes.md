Overall `CANDIDATE_ACCEPTED`。

建議改寫：TSMC named ASML, Applied Materials, Lam Research, and Tokyo Electron among its suppliers, including long-time suppliers providing tools for TSMC Arizona in 2022; ASML describes its EUV lithography technology as unique to ASML.

來源限制：
- The supplier evidence establishes named supplier relationships and collaboration, not supplier rankings, exclusivity, procurement concentration, or a specific share of TSMC purchases.
- The EUV exclusivity evidence is ASML's own description of its technology; it does not imply ASML is the exclusive supplier of all lithography or semiconductor equipment.

Focused research found official TSMC supplier disclosures for all named companies and official ASML evidence for EUV uniqueness; no unresolved atoms remain.
