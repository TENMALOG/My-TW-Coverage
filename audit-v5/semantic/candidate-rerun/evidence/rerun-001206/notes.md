## 判定

Overall `PARTIAL`。

建議改寫：威睿表示，其客戶包括全球知名電信公司、網路服務營運商、政府機關、教育／學術機構及大型企業；年報明確稱電信網路營運商為主要客戶，但未證實其餘類別各自均屬主要客戶。

來源限制：
- The evidence for customer categories is issuer-reported, not independent customer-by-customer verification.
- The 2024 annual report identifies customer types but does not provide category-level sales shares or rankings for government, academic, or large-enterprise customers.

Official issuer profile and the latest located official annual report support the customer categories, but do not substantiate the blanket qualifier that every listed category is a major customer group. The unresolved qualifier is retained as UNKNOWN.
