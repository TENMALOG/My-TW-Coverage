Overall `CANDIDATE_ACCEPTED`。

建議改寫：Cochlear was the leading global cochlear-implant brand/company in FY2026, with Cochlear estimating over 60% global share of the cochlear-implant market.

來源限制：
- The latest quantified share located is Cochlear's own estimate; the cited chart does not state whether the share is based on units or revenue.
- The evidence supports leadership in the global cochlear-implant market, not the broader market for hearing devices or all implantable hearing solutions.
- The issuer reports company-level market share; it does not separately quantify trademark-level share. MarketsandMarkets provides secondary corroboration of Cochlear's 2023 market leadership.

Focused searches found current official FY2026 market-share disclosure and secondary ranking corroboration; remaining uncertainty is limited to the issuer's undisclosed share-measurement basis and the company-versus-brand distinction.
