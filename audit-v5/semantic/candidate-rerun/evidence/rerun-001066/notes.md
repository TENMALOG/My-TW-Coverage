# 重驗筆記 — 聯嘉投控 / 3717:material_named_relationship:e3d253a6e990043a

原始 claim：**下游**：交貨予國際 [[Tier 1]] 車燈總成廠 (如 Magna, Hella) 或直接供應 [[車廠]] (OEM)。

## 原子主張

1. `SUPPORTED` — 聯嘉光電向國際 Tier 1 車燈供應商供貨；Magna、Forvia-Hella（Hella）為其客戶例子。
   期間：FY2025 customer disclosure; corroborating report dated 2025-10-01
   說明：Issuer materials identify Magna and Forvia-Hella among the company’s top five customers and describe supplying Tier-1 plants with lighting-source solutions. A secondary report specifically characterizes Magna and Hella as Tier-1 automotive lighting suppliers. This supports the qualitative relationship; no customer-specific sales, shipment quantity, or revenue denominator is disclosed here.
   來源：[2025年聯嘉第四季法說會簡報（中文）](https://www.eoi.com.tw/PDFs/Investor/2025%E5%B9%B4%E8%81%AF%E5%98%89%E7%AC%AC%E5%9B%9B%E5%AD%A3%E6%B3%95%E8%AA%AA%E6%9C%83%E7%B0%A1%E5%A0%B1_%E4%B8%AD%E6%96%87.pdf)；定位：Pages 12–13: labels Magna and Forvia-Hella among 聯嘉光電前五大客戶; gives Forvia-Hella’s global lighting-sales rank.
   來源：[聯嘉投控（3717）年報2025](https://financialfilings.com/filings/eoi-holding-3717/annual-report/2026/48011304/)；定位：Annual report, printed page 106: identifies OE automotive manufacturers as the group’s main service customers and lists design, development, certification, testing, and mass-production services; page 96 describes solutions supplied to automakers.
   來源：[聯嘉兩岸產能塞爆](https://money.udn.com/money/story/5710/9039159)；定位：Article identifies Magna and Hella as North American Tier-1 automotive-lighting manufacturers and describes EOI as a long-term supplier.
2. `SUPPORTED` — 聯嘉光電直接服務汽車原廠（OEM/OE）客戶。
   期間：FY2025
   說明：The issuer’s annual report calls OE automakers the group’s main service customers and says it provides design, development, certification, testing, and mass-production services. It also describes supplying solutions to automakers. This supports a direct OEM/OE customer relationship; the report does not disclose a separate direct-sales volume or customer-specific share.
   來源：[聯嘉投控（3717）年報2025](https://financialfilings.com/filings/eoi-holding-3717/annual-report/2026/48011304/)；定位：Annual report, printed page 106: identifies OE automotive manufacturers as the group’s main service customers and lists design, development, certification, testing, and mass-production services; page 96 describes solutions supplied to automakers.

## 判定

Overall `CANDIDATE_ACCEPTED`。

建議改寫：車用照明業務方面，聯嘉光電以 Tier-2／Tier-3 供應商角色，向國際 Tier-1 車燈供應商（包括 Magna、Forvia-Hella）提供產品，並以 OE 汽車原廠為主要服務對象，提供設計、開發、認證、測試及量產服務。

來源限制：
- 年報將聯嘉光電定位為 Tier-2／Tier-3，而非 Tier-1；此處 Tier-1 指其下游客戶。
- 客戶關係有定性披露，未找到客戶別銷售額、出貨量或營收占比；不應據此推算重要性或占比。
- 2025年法說簡報列出 Magna、Forvia-Hella 為前五大客戶；具體 Tier-1 身分另有次級報導佐證。

已查核2025年 issuer 法說簡報、2025年年報及次級報導，兩個原子主張均有支持；無需以鄰近年度或未披露的銷售分母補足。
