## 判定

Overall `PARTIAL`。

建議改寫：In FY2025, overseas sales were reported as 98.03% of total sales, and U.S. sales as 90.77%; the issuer describes North America as a major market, but the available geographic breakdown does not establish that 99% or more of exports went to North America.

來源限制：
- The FY2025 annual-report copy was located on a secondary filing-hosting site, not retrieved from the issuer's archive. Its geographic table has an internal inconsistency in the Others row and subtotal.
- The issuer's 2024 sustainability report supports the qualitative statements that products are almost entirely exported and North America is a main market; it does not support a 99% North America destination share.

The latest full-year regional disclosure located does not separately report North America sales or resolve the export-sales denominator. The exact 99%+ North America share remains unresolved.
