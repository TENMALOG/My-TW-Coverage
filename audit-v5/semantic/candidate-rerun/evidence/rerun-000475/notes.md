# 重驗筆記 — 世界健身-KY / 2762:objective_ranking_or_market_share:d7e0098f0ada0257

原始 claim：世界健身-KY (2762，World Gym Taiwan) 是台灣市佔率第一的連鎖健身品牌，會員數超過 45 萬人，全台據點逾 100 間。

## 原子主張

1. `SUPPORTED` — 世界健身-KY（World Gym Taiwan）是台灣市佔率第一的連鎖健身品牌。
   期間：2023 market-share data, reported in a 2024 issuer presentation; the TWSE listing profile separately describes the company as Taiwan's first-ranked fitness chain.
   說明：The issuer presentation reports Taiwan market shares of 55% by revenue and 49% by members for World Gym, ahead of the named competitor and others. It therefore supports the first-place ranking. The presentation's footnote says market shares are estimated by 2023 revenue, so the precise basis for the separate “by members” figures is not fully clear.
   來源：[World Gym Presentation](https://investor.worldgymtaiwan.com/files/uploads/shareholders/World%20Gym%20company%20presentation_20240829_EN_ver9.pdf)；定位：Taiwan Competitive Landscape slide, footnote 1.
   來源：[新上市公司：世界健身-KY(2762)](https://www.twse.com.tw/market_insights/zh/detail/ff8080818cc7b692018cf223864400e5)；定位：Opening company description.
2. `UNKNOWN` — 會員數超過45萬人。
   期間：Not established for a specific date.
   說明：The company's About page displays “450,000+” total members, but gives no effective date or definition of the count; another bullet on that page associates 450,000 members with 2022. The issuer's 2024 presentation describes the count as approximately 450,000 as of August 2024. This evidence does not firmly establish a dated count strictly greater than 450,000 or clarify whether it means active or another membership measure.
   來源：[關於World Gym｜全台第一大健身品牌介紹｜World Gym世界健身俱樂部](https://www.worldgymtaiwan.com/about-us)；定位：“台灣最大上市健身品牌” section; branch-count bullet and “俱樂部總數” metric.
   來源：[World Gym Presentation](https://investor.worldgymtaiwan.com/files/uploads/shareholders/World%20Gym%20company%20presentation_20240829_EN_ver9.pdf)；定位：Taiwan Competitive Landscape slide, footnote 1.
3. `SUPPORTED` — 全台據點逾100間。
   期間：Company webpage references 2025 and also lists a current-looking club total without an as-of date; the TWSE profile gives an earlier count of over 118.
   說明：Official company and TWSE sources both establish more than 100 Taiwan locations. The company page lists 145 branches in one bullet and 146 clubs in another; either figure supports the stated threshold, but the page does not reconcile the discrepancy.

   來源：[新上市公司：世界健身-KY(2762)](https://www.twse.com.tw/market_insights/zh/detail/ff8080818cc7b692018cf223864400e5)；定位：Opening company description.

## 判定

Overall `PARTIAL`。

建議改寫：官方資料支持世界健身-KY在2023年台灣健身連鎖市場排名第一（公司簡報所列市占率以2023年營收估算），且全台據點逾100間。會員數超過45萬人仍需有明確統計時點及口徑的資料確認。

來源限制：
- 市占率排名有官方交易所介紹及公司簡報佐證；簡報揭露的市占率為公司估算，並註明以2023年營收估算。
- 公司官網會員總數顯示450,000+，但頁面未標明該數字的有效日期或計數口徑；因此不能據此確認特定時點嚴格超過45萬人。
- 公司官網同頁列出145家分店及146家俱樂部，數字不一致，但都支持據點逾100間。

Focused searches located official exchange, issuer-presentation, and company-webpage evidence for the ranking and location threshold. The remaining member-count evidence lacks a clear effective date and counting definition, so that atom remains UNKNOWN.
