# 重驗筆記 — 永豐金 / 2890:profitability_assertion:87eb0db69c8e92ab

原始 claim：[[永豐金融控股]] (2890) 擁有雙獲利引擎：[[永豐銀行]]及[[永豐金證券]]。

## 原子主張

1. `SUPPORTED` — 永豐銀行 is one of 永豐金控’s two bank-and-securities profit engines.
   期間：FY2025
   說明：The issuer’s company profile labels its operating model「銀證雙引擎」and reports 永豐銀行 as 70% of subsidiary profit contribution for 2025. The denominator is the issuer’s subsidiary profit contribution, not total revenue or a market share; no external market definition applies. The issuer separately reported the bank’s 2025 after-tax net profit as NT$19.46 billion.
   來源：[永豐金控 2025年第四季公司簡介](https://www.sinopac.com/upload/sinopac/spfcMenu/19cd142885e0000013e7.pdf)；定位：PDF p. 5, slide 6,「銀證雙引擎的營運模式」and「各子公司獲利佔比」
   來源：[永豐金控 2025年第四季營運報告](https://www.sinopac.com/investors/20211210153457585149/20211210153458742624/20260309141442543244.html)；定位：2025年第四季營運報告, paragraphs on 永豐金證券
2. `SUPPORTED` — 永豐金證券 is one of 永豐金控’s two bank-and-securities profit engines.
   期間：FY2025
   說明：The issuer’s company profile labels its operating model「銀證雙引擎」and reports 永豐金證券 as 23% of subsidiary profit contribution for 2025. The denominator is the issuer’s subsidiary profit contribution, not total revenue or a market share; no external market definition applies. The issuer separately reported the securities subsidiary’s 2025 after-tax net profit as NT$6.47 billion.
   來源：[永豐金控 2025年第四季公司簡介](https://www.sinopac.com/upload/sinopac/spfcMenu/19cd142885e0000013e7.pdf)；定位：PDF p. 5, slide 6,「銀證雙引擎的營運模式」and「各子公司獲利佔比」
   來源：[永豐金控 2025年第四季營運報告](https://www.sinopac.com/investors/20211210153457585149/20211210153458742624/20260309141442543244.html)；定位：2025年第四季營運報告, paragraphs on 永豐金證券

## 判定

Overall `CANDIDATE_ACCEPTED`。

建議改寫：永豐金控將永豐銀行與永豐金證券定位為「銀證雙引擎」；其2025年各子公司獲利貢獻中，永豐銀行占70%、永豐金證券占23%。

來源限制：
- The issuer’s 2025 profit-contribution breakdown also includes 京城銀行 at 4% and other subsidiaries at 3%;「雙獲利引擎」is supported as a description of the two principal engines, not as a claim that they are the group’s only profit contributors.

Sufficient official issuer evidence supports both engines and the issuer’s dual-engine characterization; stopped before the research-turn limit.
