## 判定

Overall `PARTIAL`。

建議改寫：世鎧精密成立於1992年，地址位於台灣高雄市岡山區。公司自述為台灣最大出口不鏽鋼與合金鋼複合螺絲及複合螺栓的生產工廠；其是否為台灣最大複合螺絲製造商，以及是否屬高雄扣件產業聚落的重要成員，尚未由所查資料獨立證實。

來源限制：
- The largest-manufacturer assertion is an issuer self-description, not an independently substantiated ranking; the source’s export and product qualifications do not fully match the claim’s broader wording.
- The company’s Kaohsiung address is verified, but the official location pages reviewed do not expressly identify that address as its headquarters.
- Evidence that the firm operates in the cluster does not by itself prove its asserted importance within it.

Focused searches covered issuer disclosures, government and registry sources, and secondary reporting. The unresolved ranking and importance assertions lack a defined denominator or direct comparative evidence; further searches within the permitted research scope did not resolve them.
