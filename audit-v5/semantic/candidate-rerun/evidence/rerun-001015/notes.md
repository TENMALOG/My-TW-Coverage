## 判定

Overall `UNKNOWN`。

建議改寫：A 2019 industry report described Samsung and Hisense as AUO's main customers in the TV-panel context, referring to 2018 supply estimates; current major-customer status is unverified.

來源限制：
- The claim gives no time period, product market, or definition of 'major customer.'
- The named-customer evidence is historical secondary analysis about TV panels, not a current issuer disclosure of company-wide customer revenue.
- AUO's 2024 annual report says no individual customer exceeded 10% of net sales in 2023 or 2024, but does not identify customers; that threshold alone neither confirms nor contradicts the broader wording.

Current official disclosure reviewed, but it does not name customers. The available named-customer evidence is historical and scoped to TV panels, so it cannot resolve the claim as written.
