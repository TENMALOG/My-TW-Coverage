## 原子主張

1. `SUPPORTED` — 公準專長於高難度材料加工。
   期間：Capability described in the issuer’s 2023-12-01 presentation; the 2025 annual report also describes its machining operations and materials.
   說明：The issuer’s presentation describes precision machining and special processes, and says its waterjet cutting system can process hard-to-machine materials without stress (presentation, pp. 3–5, 9). The 2025 annual report lists titanium alloy among production materials and describes precision-machined equipment components. This supports the stated capability; no specific difficult-material product volumes or revenue denominator are given, and those are not required by this qualitative claim.

## 判定

Overall `CANDIDATE_ACCEPTED`。

建議改寫：公司專長於高難度材料加工與潔淨室組裝，成功打入全球半導體設備與低軌衛星供應鏈。

來源限制：
- The difficult-material and cleanroom claims are supported by issuer-presented capabilities; the sources do not quantify related output.
- The issuer confirms satellite-chain entry but does not specify LEO or name the customer in the cited official timeline. LEO/SpaceX specificity and shipment status rely on contemporaneous secondary reporting.
- The claim is qualitative, so no market denominator or measurement unit applies. No market-share or revenue inference is made.

The atomic wording is supported by issuer disclosures and contemporaneous reporting; remaining limits concern the issuer’s lack of public customer-specific LEO shipment detail, not an unresolved contradiction.
