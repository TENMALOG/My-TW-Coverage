原始 claim：[[Nike]] (耐吉，佔營收約 85%~90%，為其全球六分之一產能供應商暨唯一授權氣墊工廠)

## 原子主張

1. `UNKNOWN` — Nike accounts for approximately 85%–90% of Feng Tay’s revenue.
   期間：Unspecified in the claim. A secondary report gives a historical figure for 2021.
   說明：A 2022 secondary research article reports that Nike accounted for 87% of Feng Tay’s total revenue in 2021. That is within the stated range, but it does not establish the share for an unspecified or current period. The cited period and denominator are therefore not sufficient to support the claim as written.
   來源：[【研究報告】豐泰(9910) 5月營運亮眼 長線成長動能續強 全年營收可望年增40%](https://cmnews.com.tw/article/3369-8eea64b5-d8c8-11ef-a574-a8000dbe5c24)；定位：Section “豐泰為國內製鞋大廠 NIKE營收佔比近9成”
2. `UNKNOWN` — Feng Tay supplies approximately one-sixth of Nike’s global production capacity.
   期間：Claim does not specify a measurement period.
   說明：Secondary reporting describes Feng Tay’s overall athletic-footwear shipments as about one-sixth of Nike’s total sales volume. That is a shipment/sales-volume comparison, not a measure of one-sixth of Nike’s installed or available production capacity, so it does not verify the wording as given.
   來源：[豐泰雙引擎齊發動 營運動能升溫 近期法人大買](https://money.udn.com/money/story/5710/9064022)；定位：Article passage stating Feng Tay’s overall footwear shipments are about one-sixth of Nike’s total sales volume

   期間：Current issuer profile; the profile does not specify an effective date. Also described in the issuer’s 2024 sustainability report.
   說明：Feng Tay’s official profile says it operates the only authorized factory producing air soles for its primary customer, Nike. The issuer’s 2024 sustainability report also describes it as the primary customer’s only authorized airbag-producing factory.
   來源：[About - Feng Tay Group Profile](https://www.fengtay.com/en/about/detail/Feng_Tay_Group_Profile)；定位：Group profile, final paragraph
   來源：[2024 豐泰企業永續報告書](https://www.fengtay.com/uploads/images/csr/report/2024ESGreport_cn.pdf)；定位：Section 1.1 集團簡介

## 判定

Overall `PARTIAL`。

建議改寫：Feng Tay’s official profile describes it as operating Nike’s only authorized air-sole production factory. Secondary reports have cited historical Nike revenue shares and an approximately one-sixth share of Nike footwear sales volume, but the revenue claim needs a specified period and the one-sixth figure should not be stated as a share of Nike production capacity without matching evidence.

來源限制：
- The 85%–90% revenue range has no period in the original wording; the available 87% figure is secondary reporting for 2021 and cannot establish the share for other periods.
- The one-sixth figure found in secondary reporting concerns Feng Tay footwear shipment volume relative to Nike sales volume, not Nike installed production capacity.
- The exclusive air-sole/airbag factory statement is an issuer self-description; no independent corroboration of the exclusivity was located.

Focused searches found issuer support for the authorized air-sole factory and historical secondary evidence for related revenue and shipment-volume figures. They did not resolve the revenue share for a specified period or establish the one-sixth production-capacity denominator; those atoms remain UNKNOWN.
