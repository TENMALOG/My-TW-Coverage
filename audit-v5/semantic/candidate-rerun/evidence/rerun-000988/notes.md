   說明：A secondary report states this specific relationship. Neither report quantifies supplier share or defines the scope behind “main supplier.”
   來源：[勤誠受惠AWS AI伺服器需求 今年營運動能強勁](https://m.cnyes.com/news/print/5864477)；定位：Article paragraph 3.

## 判定

Overall `CANDIDATE_ACCEPTED`。

建議改寫：As reported on February 17, 2025, Chenbro was a major supplier of HGX AI server chassis for AWS; the issuer’s public disclosures reviewed here do not independently confirm the AWS-specific designation or quantify what “major” means.

來源限制：
- The AWS-specific HGX designation is supported by secondary reporting, not by an issuer disclosure located in this review.
- Chenbro’s CEO separately confirmed HGX chassis supply and continued shipments, without identifying AWS as the customer.
- “Main/major supplier” is not accompanied by a market-share figure, ranking, or defined supplier universe.

The exact AWS–HGX supplier wording was located in secondary reporting, with separate issuer-attributed corroboration for HGX chassis supply generally. Further searching was stopped; the AWS-specific wording and undefined supplier scope remain matters for final adjudication.
