Overall `PARTIAL`。

建議改寫：CyberPower's issuer-disclosed, NPD-attributed ranking was second in the broader North American UPS market in 2023. The available disclosure does not establish approximately 15% share, a below-20-kVA ranking, or that APC/Schneider Electric was the only brand ahead.

來源限制：
- The NPD data underlying the issuer's 2023 statement was not available in the located disclosure; the denominator, capacity coverage, and market-channel coverage are not specified there.
- The approximately 15% and below-20-kVA details appear in a user-authored article without a traceable citation for those figures.
- The issuer's 2020 statement of fourth place worldwide is historical and does not establish the undated global-leadership claim as current.

Focused searches and source retrieval found an issuer-attributed 2023 second-place ranking, but no reliable evidence resolving the claim's 15% share, below-20-kVA scope, named competitor ordering, or current global-ranking wording. Unresolved atoms remain UNKNOWN.
