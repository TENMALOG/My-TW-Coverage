## 判定

Overall `PARTIAL`。

建議改寫：Secondary sources report that 劍麟 has long partnered with Autoliv, ZF, and Joyson Electronics in automotive safety components, and describe them as leading global airbag-system suppliers. The specific identification of Joyson Safety Systems, and the current named-customer status of all three, is not confirmed by the issuer’s latest report, which anonymizes significant customers.

來源限制：
- The issuer’s 2025 consolidated financial report identifies significant customers only as Groups A, B, and C; it does not disclose their names.
- Named customer relationships come from secondary reporting, not from a current issuer disclosure naming those entities.
- Secondary sources say Joyson Electronics/均勝電子; they do not establish Joyson Safety Systems as the precise customer entity.
- The global-three characterization is supported for airbag-system suppliers, not independently established as a ranking across the broader automotive-safety Tier 1 market. The reported combined market share lacks a stated measurement methodology and units.

Focused searches and review of the latest issuer financial report and corroborating secondary reports completed; the exact Joyson Safety Systems entity and current named-customer identities remain undisclosed by the issuer.
