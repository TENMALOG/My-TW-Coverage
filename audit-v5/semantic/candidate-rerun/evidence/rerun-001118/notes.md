## 判定

Overall `UNKNOWN`。

來源限制：
- The claim provides no date or definition of “主要供應商” (for example, by purchase value, volume, or approved-vendor status).
- The issuer sources reviewed do not provide supplier-specific rebar procurement data sufficient to determine whether 中鋼 was a main supplier.
- The named-supplier list is evidence of other identified suppliers, not conclusive evidence that 中鋼 was not also a supplier.

Focused issuer-source research found no disclosure identifying 中鋼 as a main rebar supplier or giving supplier-level rebar purchasing amounts or rankings. The remaining scope and denominator are unresolved.
