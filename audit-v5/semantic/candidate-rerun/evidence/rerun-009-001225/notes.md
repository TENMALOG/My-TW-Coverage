## 判定

Overall `PARTIAL`。

建議改寫：A secondary analyst report states that TSMC accounted for about 60% of 台特化's 2024 revenue; the issuer's 2023 annual report reports a 59.63% share for an unnamed largest customer. 台特化 received a TSMC supplier award in 2019, reportedly in its first year of supply. A 2022 feature reports historical orders from Texas Instruments. Supply to 世界先進 (VIS), and whether TI supply is current, remain unverified.

來源限制：
- The issuer's annual report anonymizes major customers, so its 59.63% figure cannot by itself identify TSMC.
- The 2024 TSMC revenue-share attribution comes from a secondary analyst report hosted on Scribd, not a named-customer issuer filing.
- The first-year award characterization comes from contemporaneous secondary reporting; the issuer's report confirms the award but not that timing.
- The TI evidence supports historical orders only; current supply status is not shown.
- The issuer's presentation describes unnamed tier-one customer qualifications and does not establish a VIS supply relationship.

Stopped after 8 focused research turns, the stated limit. The VIS relationship remains unresolved, and the available primary issuer customer-concentration disclosure does not name the largest customer.
