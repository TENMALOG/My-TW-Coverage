## 判定

Overall `CANDIDATE_ACCEPTED`。

建議改寫：Issuer materials report Honda as 豐祥-KY’s largest customer in FY2024, at 32.37% of sales, and identify Polaris and Ducati among its principal supply relationships. Yamaha is documented in the issuer’s 2018 Q3 customer table; Harley-Davidson is described as a customer in June 2023 secondary reporting. The evidence does not establish all five names as a contemporaneous customer roster.

來源限制：
- The original wording provides no reference period for the customer list. Supporting evidence spans different periods, so it cannot establish that every named company was a customer at the same time.
- Honda’s largest-customer status is supported for FY2024 specifically; it should not be generalized to every period.
- The Yamaha evidence is historical (2018 Q3). The Harley-Davidson evidence is secondary reporting from 2023 and does not confirm current status.
- Customer sales percentages are issuer sales mix, not market share in any end market.
