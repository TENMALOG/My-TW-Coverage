## 判定

Overall `PARTIAL`。

建議改寫：Cochlear reported an issuer-estimated global share above 60% in FY24 for hearing devices treating severe-or-higher hearing loss. Separately, 2026 secondary reporting says 鈺鎧 supplied a wireless-charging module for over five years into the supply chain of an unnamed cochlear-implant producer estimated to have approximately 70% global share. The available evidence does not establish that the unnamed producer is Cochlear.

來源限制：
- Cochlear's market-share evidence is issuer-estimated and applies to a specified severe-or-higher hearing-loss segment, not all hearing devices; the share calculation basis is not given in the cited material.
- The supplier evidence is secondary and does not name the implant maker or verify that it is Cochlear.
- 鈺鎧's annual-report customer table uses anonymized customer labels; that disclosure cannot identify the unnamed supplier relationship.

Focused searches and review of issuer disclosures, the listing prospectus, Cochlear's official FY24 materials, and secondary reporting found support for Cochlear's leadership in a defined implant-related market but no reliable source naming Cochlear as 鈺鎧's customer. The named supplier relationship remains unresolved.
