## 判定

Overall `PARTIAL`。

建議改寫：Delta provides power solutions for AI servers, offers EV-charging products, and has industrial-automation offerings. Delta describes itself as the world’s largest switching-power-supply provider, but the reviewed evidence does not establish the ranking’s market definition, measurement basis, denominator, or period.

來源限制：
- The “world’s largest” statement is an issuer self-description; the reviewed official materials do not provide a dated, defined market ranking or its denominator.
- The FY2024 annual report and product pages support the listed business activities, not market leadership in AI servers, EV charging, or industrial automation.

Focused searches found official disclosures supporting the three business-activity atoms and an issuer statement of global largest-provider status. The ranking remains unresolved because the available assertion lacks the market scope, denominator, metric, and effective period needed to verify an objective ranking.
