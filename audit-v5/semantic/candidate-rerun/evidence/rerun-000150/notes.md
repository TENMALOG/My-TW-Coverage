## 判定

Overall `PARTIAL`。

建議改寫：智易科技（3596；Arcadyan）隸屬仁寶集團，專注於用戶端接取／路由產品及寬頻網通解決方案。

來源限制：
- The issuer's own product descriptions establish its product scope and self-description, not an independent market ranking.
- The 2017 issuer disclosure ranks IAD shipments in the global top three; that historical, shipment-based statement neither establishes first place nor disproves it.
- The secondary global-largest IAD claim lacks a stated period, market definition, denominator, and methodology.
- The issuer's global-leading Wi-Fi CPE description covers a narrower product category than the claim's broad broadband networking equipment wording.

Focused searches of issuer disclosures, annual reports, and secondary coverage found no period-specific, independently verifiable global IAD ranking or market-share measure matching the claim's scope. The unresolved ranking atoms remain UNKNOWN.
