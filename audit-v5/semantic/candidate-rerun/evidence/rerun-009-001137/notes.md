## 判定

Overall `PARTIAL`。

建議改寫：台鋼新零售（3085）於2025年6月5日更名，前身公司名稱為創新新零售；公司自述為台鋼集團投資之資訊服務公司，且其2025年第一季財報列有電子商務平台服飾買賣業務。

來源限制：
- Group affiliation is supported by the issuer's own description; the cited page does not specify ownership percentage.
- The B2C apparel-e-commerce label appears in 2024 issuer-hosted material, while the 2025 Q1 filing confirms an apparel e-commerce segment but not the current B2C designation.
- The company’s current issuer description includes broader retail and information-services activities, so the narrower B2C apparel identity should not be inferred from the apparel segment alone.

Focused searches found primary issuer and registry evidence for the corporate identity, group affiliation, apparel e-commerce segment, prior name, and 2025 name change. Current-period evidence did not fully establish the B2C apparel-e-commerce characterization, so that atom remains UNKNOWN.
