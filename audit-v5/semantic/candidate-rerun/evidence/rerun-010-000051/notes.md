來源限制：
- The 2022 customer details and 2014 market-share estimate are secondary sources; the latter is an author calculation and applies only to 2014.
- The 2025 summary that mentions approximately 90% is LLM-generated and describes AM channel mix, not North American market share.
- No located source supplies a period-matched North American AM TPMS market denominator and 為升 sales figure supporting the stated 90% share.

Focused searches did not locate a reliable issuer or regulator disclosure establishing the claimed 90% North American AM TPMS market share or the specific SMP/Wells-to-TPMS link. The available 90% reference has a different denominator, and the historical 2014 estimate cannot resolve the claim's unstated period.
