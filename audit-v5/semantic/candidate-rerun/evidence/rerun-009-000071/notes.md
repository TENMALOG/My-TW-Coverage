來源限制：
- The most specific evidence for Cummins's customer ranking, the four OEM customer relationships, and their market-share figures is secondary reporting, not an issuer filing or a primary customer-level disclosure.
- The issuer's website identifies Cummins as a main customer and calls it the world's largest diesel-engine manufacturer, but does not establish Cummins's North America-specific leadership or its rank among the issuer's customers.
- The 2024 article does not state the period, truck class, or denominator underlying the 30%, 20%, and 10% figures. A separate North American Class 6–8 top-four report confirms a relevant manufacturer set but cannot validate those percentages.

Focused searches found secondary reporting for the customer and OEM claims, but no primary source establishing the claimed indirect component-flow path or market-share figures with a defined period and denominator. Remaining scope gaps are marked UNKNOWN.
