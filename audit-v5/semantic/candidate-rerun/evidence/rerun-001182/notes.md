# 重驗筆記 — 久元 / 6261:material_named_relationship:25564dd7283d8c8e

原始 claim：主要客戶[[晶元光電]]占營收 23.5%。

## 原子主張

1. `SUPPORTED` — 晶元光電 was listed as a major customer of 久元.
   期間：2011 Q1
   說明：久元's prospectus lists customers that accounted for at least 10% of sales and identifies 晶元光電 for the quarter ended March 31, 2011. The disclosure is issuer-level, in NT$ thousands; it is not evidence of a current relationship.
   來源：[久元電子股份有限公司 2011年國內第三次無擔保轉換公司債公開說明書](https://www.ytec.com.tw/en/investor/prospectus/download/MP/Draft%20of%20Prospectus%20for%20the%203rd%20Issance%20of%20Unsecured%20Domestic%20Convertible%20Corporate%20Bonds%20by%20YTEK%20in%202011.pdf)；定位：Page 51, section 5(2)
2. `SUPPORTED` — 晶元光電 accounted for 23.5% of 久元's revenue.
   期間：2011 Q1
   說明：The prospectus reports sales of NT$173,491 thousand for 晶元光電, 23.50% of net sales of NT$738,417 thousand for the quarter ended March 31, 2011. This is a historical issuer-level sales denominator, not a current-period measure.
   來源：[久元電子股份有限公司 2011年國內第三次無擔保轉換公司債公開說明書](https://www.ytec.com.tw/en/investor/prospectus/download/MP/Draft%20of%20Prospectus%20for%20the%203rd%20Issance%20of%20Unsecured%20Domestic%20Convertible%20Corporate%20Bonds%20by%20YTEK%20in%202011.pdf)；定位：Page 51, section 5(2)
3. `UNKNOWN` — The stated 23.5% applies to the claim's intended, unspecified period.
   期間：Unspecified
   說明：The claim gives no period. The verified disclosure establishes 23.50% only for 2011 Q1; it does not establish that the same share applies to another or current period.
   來源：[久元電子股份有限公司 2011年國內第三次無擔保轉換公司債公開說明書](https://www.ytec.com.tw/en/investor/prospectus/download/MP/Draft%20of%20Prospectus%20for%20the%203rd%20Issance%20of%20Unsecured%20Domestic%20Convertible%20Corporate%20Bonds%20by%20YTEK%20in%202011.pdf)；定位：Page 51, section 5(2)

## 判定

Overall `PARTIAL`。

建議改寫：久元電子於2011年第一季的公開說明書列示，晶元光電銷貨淨額為新台幣173,491仟元，占該季銷貨淨額新台幣738,417仟元的23.50%。

來源限制：
- The primary source is 久元's company prospectus, printed August 3, 2011.
- The 23.50% figure is explicitly for the quarter ended March 31, 2011 and uses net sales as the denominator.
- The disclosure does not support treating 23.5% as a current or period-independent customer revenue share.

Research located direct issuer disclosure confirming the customer and exact percentage for 2011 Q1. The claim omits its period, and the evidence does not resolve any other or current period; further research cannot determine which period the original wording intended.
