## 判定

Overall `PARTIAL`。

建議改寫：永捷 lists PU synthetic resin and PU adhesives among its products; its website describes applications involving synthetic leather and shoes. The approximately 40% figure and a supply relationship through 寶成/豐泰 to Nike/adidas remain unverified.

來源限制：
- The issuer's product disclosures support general product categories and applications, not the full sports-shoe or “traditional business” characterization.
- The 40% figure was found only in a secondary source, with unclear scope and methodology.
- Evidence that 寶成 and 豐泰 manufacture for named brands does not establish that 永捷 supplies either manufacturer.
- The issuer's public product pages do not provide a period-specific denominator for the claimed 40%.

Focused searches found official issuer product disclosures and official manufacturer descriptions, but no reliable primary evidence for the 40% figure or the claimed supplier chain. Further results located were secondary or did not resolve those gaps.
