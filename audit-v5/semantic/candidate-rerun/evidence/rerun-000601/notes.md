來源限制：
- The leadership descriptions are issuer or partner-profile statements, not independent market rankings; no global market denominator, measured share, or ranking method was disclosed.
- Evidence for IP leadership applies to specified HBM and die-to-die products, not to the broader claim that GUC is a globally leading IP supplier.

Official company and TSMC disclosures support ASIC-service positioning and TSMC's largest-shareholder status, but targeted searches found no reliable global ranking or market denominator supporting the broader IP-supplier leadership wording.
