Actual 2021 shipments and global exclusivity are not independently verified here.

- The relevant customer and order details come from secondary media reports attributed to industry sources, not identified issuer or customer disclosures.

- The global-uniqueness assertion has no stated supplier universe, denominator, model scope, or comparison methodology.

Focused searches found contemporaneous secondary reports for the customer/order assertions but no primary confirmation or documented global comparison for uniqueness; the exclusivity atom therefore remains UNKNOWN.
