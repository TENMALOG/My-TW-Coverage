## 判定

Overall `PARTIAL`。

建議改寫：2924's Cayman issuer, formerly 開曼東凌股份有限公司, was established on 2009-02-06 and legally changed its name to 開曼群島宏太股份有限公司 in November 2022. Its 宏太-KY short name/new shares began OTC trading on 2023-01-16. The issuer's history traces the Taiwan 東凌股份有限公司 business to 1988.

來源限制：
- The 1988 date applies to the Taiwan predecessor/group history, not the Cayman issuer's establishment.
- The 2023 date is supported for the market short-name/trading change; the formal Cayman legal-name registration was in 2022.
- The trading-date source is a secondary site reproducing the issuer's public announcement; the issuer's sustainability report independently supports the 2022 legal-name-change year.

Official issuer sustainability report and the reproduced issuer announcement resolve the relevant entity, establishment, and name-change dates; no further focused search was needed.
