# 重驗筆記 — 欣高 / 9931:material_named_relationship:73633ab28e6947bb

   期間：ROC years 112–113 (2023–2024) in the annual-report supplier table; the report also describes the company’s gas sales as supplied by CPC.
   說明：The issuer’s report identifies 中油 (CPC) as the supplier and says the company’s gas sales are supplied by CPC. This supports the supplier relationship, not exclusivity.
   來源：[欣高石油氣股份有限公司 2024 年報（中文）](https://www.hkgas.com.tw/FileList/%E6%AC%A3%E9%AB%98%E5%85%AC%E5%8F%B8114%E5%B9%B4%28113%E5%B9%B4%29%E5%B9%B4%E5%A0%B1.pdf)；定位：Printed p. 48, subsection 1 售氣, table 最近二年度售氣主要供應商資料

2. `CONTRADICTED` — CPC is 欣高’s exclusive natural-gas supplier.
   期間：ROC years 112–113 (2023–2024), as reported; the report also includes a ROC 114 interim column.
   說明：The issuer’s supplier table reports CPC at 99.95% and “Other” at 0.05% of net purchase amount in both ROC years 112 and 113. This conflicts with exclusivity on the reported purchase-value basis. The table does not identify the other supplier(s) or establish their share by physical gas volume.
   來源：[欣高石油氣股份有限公司 2024 年報（中文）](https://www.hkgas.com.tw/FileList/%E6%AC%A3%E9%AB%98%E5%85%AC%E5%8F%B8114%E5%B9%B4%28113%E5%B9%B4%29%E5%B9%B4%E5%A0%B1.pdf)；定位：Printed p. 48, subsection 1 售氣, table 最近二年度售氣主要供應商資料
   來源：[欣高石油氣股份有限公司產品頁](https://www.hkgas.com.tw/product.aspx)；定位：三、主要原料之供應狀況

## 判定

Overall `PARTIAL`。

建議改寫：CPC is 欣高’s principal natural-gas supplier. In the issuer’s report, CPC accounted for 99.95% of net purchase amount for gas sales in ROC years 112 and 113, while “Other” accounted for 0.05%; exclusive supply by physical gas volume is not established.

來源限制：
- The supplier table measures net purchase amount, not natural-gas volume, and aggregates the residual supplier(s) as “Other.”
- The issuer’s product page is undated; its statement that CPC is the main source supports primacy, not exclusivity.
- The 2024 annual report’s interim column is labeled ROC 114 through the previous quarter; the annual supplier percentages cited here are limited to ROC years 112–113.

Stopped after 8 focused research/tool turns. The official issuer report supports CPC as supplier but reports a nonzero “Other” share by purchase value, so the complete exclusivity wording is not supported.
