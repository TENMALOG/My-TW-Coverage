## 判定

Overall `PARTIAL`。

建議改寫：In its FY2025 annual-report text, 錩新 names Harman and Pioneer among its automotive-speaker customers and describes JBL as a Harman brand in the consumer-speaker context. A 2014 secondary report also named Bose as a customer. The reviewed evidence does not establish that Harman/JBL, Pioneer, and Bose were the top three customers or verify the stated individual revenue-share ranges.

來源限制：
- The claim does not specify its period or the revenue denominator for the percentages.
- The FY2025 annual-report PDF was identified through the TWSE filing page, but direct PDF retrieval failed; the report text was available through a third-party page reproducing it.
- The 2014 report is historical. Any combined customer-share figures in older reporting cannot establish individual shares or current rankings.

Reached the eight-turn research limit. Ranking and individual-share atoms remain unresolved.
