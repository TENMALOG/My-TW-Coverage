來源限制：
- The key supplier and contract announcements are published by Ericsson, the vendor; the 2021 announcement also quotes Far EasTone’s president.
- No source found in this review quantified supplier shares by base-station count, RAN capacity, core-network equipment, or spending.
- The claim does not specify period or market scope. Public commercial mobile-network deployments and dedicated private 5G networks should not be conflated.

Focused searches found official evidence of Ericsson’s RAN and Core supplier relationships, including a 2026–2028 agreement. No evidence located defined or quantified “main supplier,” so that qualifier remains unresolved.
