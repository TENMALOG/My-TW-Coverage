# 重驗筆記 — 欣興 / 3037:objective_ranking_or_market_share:2416d3deb1742a86

原始 claim：[[欣興]]電子 ([[Unimicron Technology]]) 為全球最大 [[PCB]] 與 IC 載板製造商之一，隸屬 [[聯電]] 集團。

## 原子主張

1. `SUPPORTED` — Unimicron manufactures PCBs and IC carrier substrates.
   期間：Current company description; no publication date stated.
   說明：Unimicron identifies separate PCB and Carrier business units, supporting both product categories.
   來源：[Global Service Network](https://www.unimicron.com/en/about04.html)；定位：Opening company description
2. `SUPPORTED` — Unimicron is one of the world's largest PCB manufacturers.
   期間：2025
   說明：Prismark ranks Unimicron second among the world's largest PCB fabricators by 2025 total revenue: US$4.225 billion. This is a manufacturer-revenue ranking, not a ranking by physical output.
   來源：[Prismark Discovery Series: Global Leading PCB Companies – 2025](https://www.prismark.com/_files/ugd/950e51_7b4250411feb4f89a9c53d4a8aa5f3dd.pdf)；定位：Page 3, “Top 100 PCB Companies in 2025”; ranking methodology and Unimicron entry
3. `SUPPORTED` — Unimicron is one of the world's largest IC substrate manufacturers.
   期間：2024 market estimate
   說明：DIGITIMES reports Unimicron among the top five global IC substrate manufacturers, with a reported 16.0% market share. The article does not disclose the underlying market-share calculation or its precise denominator methodology.
   來源：[Global advanced IC substrate market set to surge to US$31.54 billion by 2029](https://www.digitimes.com/news/a20240926PD212/market-ic-substrate-data-growth-demand.html)；定位：“IC substrate key players”
4. `SUPPORTED` — Unimicron is affiliated with the UMC group.
   期間：2026
   說明：UMC calls Unimicron an invested company of the UMC group. UMC's financial statements report 13.01% ownership and account for Unimicron as an associate based on significant influence through board representation; this supports group affiliation, not a claim that Unimicron is a controlled or wholly owned subsidiary.
   來源：[UMC Announces Key Changes in Executive Leadership](https://www.umc.com/en/News/press_release/Content/corporate/20260225)；定位：Press release text describing Unimicron as “an invested company of the UMC group”
   來源：[United Microelectronics Corporation and Subsidiaries: Consolidated Financial Statements for the Three-Month Periods Ended March 31, 2026 and 2025](https://www.umc.com/upload/media/08_Investors/Financials/Financial_Statements/Financial_Statements_English_pdf/2026/2026Q1_Consolidated_Financial_Statements_e.pdf)；定位：Note 6(7), Note C, and investment ownership table

## 判定

Overall `CANDIDATE_ACCEPTED`。

建議改寫：欣興電子（Unimicron Technology）是全球主要PCB與IC載板製造商之一；Prismark列其2025年全球PCB製造商營收排名第2，DIGITIMES報導其2024年IC載板市佔估計為16.0%。欣興為聯電集團投資關係企業，並非聯電合併控制的子公司。

來源限制：
- Prismark's PCB ranking is based on total company revenue and may include activities beyond bare-board shipments for some listed companies.
- DIGITIMES is a secondary source; its reported IC substrate market-share methodology and exact denominator are not disclosed in the article.
- The UMC relationship is supported as a group investment/associate relationship. “隸屬” should not be taken to mean Unimicron is a controlled subsidiary.

The product, global scale, and UMC group-affiliation atoms were resolved with company, UMC, industry-research, and secondary reporting sources; stopped before the eight-turn research limit.
