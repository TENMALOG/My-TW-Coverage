## 判定

Overall `UNKNOWN`。

建議改寫：昭輝's 2024 and 2025 issuer presentations describe North America as 85% of its sales geography and show a separate AM-plastic-parts market-share chart, but do not establish that LKQ/Keystone is currently North America's largest AM channel or that the pair account for more than 50% of 昭輝 revenue. A 2014 secondary report attributed an approximately 50% customer share to LKQ alone, which is historical and does not verify the combined claim.

來源限制：
- The original wording gives no reference period, precise AM market definition, or revenue-share measurement basis.
- The issuer presentations' 85% figure is geographic sales mix, not customer concentration; their AM-plastic-parts chart does not identify the competitors behind its labels.
- The 2014 MoneyDJ report is secondary, attributes its information to an analyst report, and is too old and differently scoped to verify the undated combined claim.
- LKQ's report identifies separate Keystone acquisitions in 2007 and 2014; the claim's slash notation does not clarify whether it means one LKQ customer group, multiple legal entities, or additive customer shares.

Focused searches and review of issuer presentations, LKQ's official report, and the historical secondary report did not resolve either the current North American ranking or the undated combined revenue share. Further evidence would need to specify the period, market denominator, and customer/account scope.
