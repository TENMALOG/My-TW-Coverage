## 判定

Overall `PARTIAL`。

建議改寫：By September 2017, contemporaneous reporting described Taiwan Name Plate as having IC-chip operating-system R&D capability and self-developed RFID printed-antenna technology for which it had obtained a U.S. invention patent. The reports said three of Taiwan’s four named leading credit-card issuers were customers, with the company supplying more than half of new-card orders for those three; cooperation with the fourth was described as historical. The reported 35% market-share figure lacks a defined denominator and measurement period, and the evidence reviewed does not establish core credit/debit-card supplier status across all four issuers.

來源限制：
- Most business and customer assertions are from 2017 media reports attributing information to company representatives, not independently audited market data.
- The 35% figure is reported without a defined market-share denominator, unit, or calculation period.
- The bank evidence distinguishes three then-current major customer relationships from prior cooperation with the fourth; it does not establish debit-card supply or core-supplier status for all four.
- The patent record reviewed is a third-party patent database page that links to USPTO records; its title and assignee support a printed-antenna patent connection but do not independently validate every aspect of the company’s RFID technology claim.

Stopped after seven focused research turns. The remaining material gaps are the denominator and measurement period underlying the 35% figure, and evidence establishing core credit/debit-card supplier status across all four banks.
