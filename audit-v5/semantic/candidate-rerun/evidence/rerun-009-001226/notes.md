## 判定

Overall `PARTIAL`。

建議改寫：In FY2024, TSMC reportedly accounted for approximately 60% of 台特化's revenue. 台特化 silicon ethane is described as an advanced-process CVD material, and secondary reporting says 台特化 received TSMC supplier recognition in its first year of supply in 2019. The reviewed evidence does not establish 台特化 silicon-ethane deliveries specifically for TSMC's 2nm or 3nm CVD processes.

來源限制：
- The 60% revenue-share figure and general CVD description come from a brokerage report accessed through an unofficial Scribd reproduction, not a directly retrieved issuer filing.
- The issuer's public history anonymizes the 2019 award-giving manufacturer as T-company; the identification as TSMC and the 'first supply year' detail rely on secondary reporting.
- The claim gives no period for the revenue share. Evidence found anchors it to FY2024; it should not be carried forward to later periods.
- No reliable evidence reviewed establishes node-specific silicon-ethane qualification or deliveries for TSMC 2nm or 3nm CVD.

Research exhausted at the eight-turn limit; the 2nm- and 3nm-specific supply atoms remain unresolved.
