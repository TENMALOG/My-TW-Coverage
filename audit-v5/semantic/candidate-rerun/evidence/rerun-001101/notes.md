## 判定

Overall `PARTIAL`。

建議改寫：Historical 2020 reporting supports Lenovo and Dell as 威鋒電子 USB-hub customers. The issuer’s May 2026 presentation shows a Plugable hub using a VIA Labs chip, but does not establish Plugable as a major customer or establish the named customers’ current status.

來源限制：
- The named Lenovo and Dell customer evidence is secondary and dates to 2020; it does not verify present-day customer status.
- The 2026 issuer slide documents chip use in a Plugable product, not a direct customer relationship or customer importance.
- The original wording gives no reference period or definition of “major”; the available sources do not establish a consistent customer-revenue denominator across the named entities.

Focused searches found historical secondary reporting for Lenovo and Dell and an official 2026 product-use disclosure for Plugable, but no reliable evidence establishing Plugable as a major customer or confirming current major-customer status. The unresolved customer-status atom remains UNKNOWN.
