## 判定

Overall `PARTIAL`。

建議改寫：In the issuer’s 2025-11-21 investor presentation, automotive electronics represented 85% of the 2024 Electronic Business Unit product mix. The presentation summary lists Bosch and TE Connectivity as automotive-sector customers/partners and identifies oxygen-sensor terminals among automotive products. The issuer also describes itself as a qualified supplier to Tier 1 automotive customers and as active in the automotive-connector supply chain. The reviewed evidence does not establish that Bosch and TE are the company’s main suppliers, does not verify injector-related products, and does not substantiate ‘key components’ for the full list.

- The 85% figure is the 2024 Electronic Business Unit product-mix share, not a verified share of consolidated company revenue.
- The issuer’s website statements do not specify a reporting period or customer-level supply/revenue breakdown.

Focused research limit reached. Some atoms are supported, but the main-supply quantifier, injector product assertion, and ‘key components’ characterization remain unresolved.
