## 判定

Overall `PARTIAL`。

建議改寫：Period-qualified version: MoneyDJ reported in January 2025 that TSMC was among 世禾's main Taiwanese customers. An issuer filing documents past business with UMC through at least 1H 2007; UMC's current customer status is unverified.

來源限制：
- The claim has no stated as-of date. TSMC is supported by a report published in January 2025, while current continuity is not established.
- The nearly 80% revenue figure in the 2025 secondary report refers to semiconductor customers collectively, not TSMC alone; it cannot establish a customer-specific denominator or share.
- UMC's direct issuer evidence is historical and cannot be treated as proof of a present relationship.
- The issuer's investor page lists a 2025 annual report published May 18, 2026, but the available search backend did not expose its contents for checking current named customers.

Focused research found recent secondary support for TSMC and direct issuer evidence only for UMC's historical relationship. Current UMC status remains unresolved; the listed 2025 issuer annual report could not be inspected through the available retrieval tools.
