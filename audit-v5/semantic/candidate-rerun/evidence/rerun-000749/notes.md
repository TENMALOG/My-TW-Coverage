# 重驗筆記 — 創控 / 6909:objective_ranking_or_market_share:257a1dfe814cf98a

原始 claim：MiTAP被[[台積電]]認可為唯一高度推薦之實驗室級現場系統。

## 原子主張

1. `SUPPORTED` — MiTAP was recognized by TSMC as a laboratory-grade, field-deployable onsite VOC/AMC monitoring system.
   期間：2015 announcement; the recognition date is not separately stated
   說明：Tricorntech’s 2015 company announcement says TSMC certified MiTAP as its onsite AMC VOC monitoring system, and describes laboratory-grade performance in field operation. The relevant scope is TSMC’s AMC/VOC monitoring use, not all laboratory or field systems.
   來源：[創控科技榮獲台積電先進製程高度肯定獎](https://www.tricorntech.com/news-detail/39/)；定位：Subtitle and bullet: “唯一認可，可與實驗室數據比對之現地 VOC 監測系統”; “經嚴峻測試與驗證，TSMC 認證 MiTAP 為其 AMC VOC 現地監測系統。”
2. `SUPPORTED` — MiTAP was the only system awarded the TSMC “Highly Commended” honor in the relevant award context.
   期間：2015 announcement; award date/category details unstated
   說明：The issuer’s English announcement says MiTAP was “the only system awarded with the Highly Commended honor.” This supports exclusivity for that honor as reported by the issuer, not a broader claim that MiTAP was the only such system in the market. “Highly Commended” is an honor/award, not necessarily a general recommendation.
   來源：[TSMC Honors Tricorntech with the Highly Commended Award](https://www.tricorntech.com/en/news-detail/39)；定位：Headline and subtitle: “It is the only system awarded with the Highly Commended honor.”

## 判定

Overall `CANDIDATE_ACCEPTED`。

建議改寫：創控科技於2015年公告稱，台積電認證MiTAP為其AMC VOC現地監測系統；公司並稱MiTAP是唯一獲「Highly Commended」肯定、具實驗室等級現地檢測性能的系統。

來源限制：
- The supporting announcement is from Tricorntech, not TSMC; no TSMC-issued corroboration was located.
- The announcement does not specify the award category or define the denominator for “only”; the exclusivity should be limited to the reported honor, not generalized to the market.
- “Highly Commended” denotes an honor in the source and should not be broadened into a general TSMC recommendation.

The official issuer’s 2015 Chinese and English announcements directly support the recognition, lab-grade field performance, and sole-honor wording. Further primary corroboration from TSMC was not located.
