## 判定

Overall `CANDIDATE_ACCEPTED`。

建議改寫：Evergreen Marine was established on September 1, 1968. By operated container-fleet TEU capacity, it was reported as Taiwan's largest major listed liner in the 2025 comparison and ranked seventh globally in Evergreen Marine's April 2026 investor-conference disclosure.

來源限制：
- The original wording does not specify a ranking metric or date. The ranking evidence supports a fleet-capacity interpretation, not market share by revenue, cargo volume, or another denominator.
- Yang Ming's slide 15 says “As of June 2025” but its source caption reads “Alphaliner TOP 100 / June 2024.” The Taiwan comparison is therefore date-inconsistent; its listed TEU figures are reported as shown.
- The global ranking is time-sensitive and applies to the operator grouping and capacity basis used in the cited disclosures. Alphaliner describes its figures as consolidated and includes Evergreen Line and Italia Marittima in Evergreen Group.

Available official issuer disclosures and Alphaliner methodology support the founding date and a time-bounded capacity-rank interpretation; remaining scope and date caveats are recorded for final adjudication.
