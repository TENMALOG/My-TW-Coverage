## 判定

Overall `PARTIAL`。

建議改寫：A DIGITIMES report dated 2021-10-12, citing industry sources, described VPEC as the leading GaAs epiwafer supplier and said its market share exceeded IQE's by 14–15%. Current global leadership is unverified; a 2026 UAnalyze report ranked VPEC second by estimated GaAs epitaxy capacity.

來源限制：
- No comparable official issuer or regulator disclosure establishing a global GaAs epitaxy supplier ranking or market-share denominator was located.
- The 2021 DIGITIMES report is secondary and paywalled; the accessible listing does not provide the underlying measurement period or methodology.
- The 2026 UAnalyze report gives a capacity-based estimate, not a comparable disclosed market-share measure, and does not specify its full denominator or measurement date.

Focused research found a 2021 report supporting historical leadership and a 2026 capacity-based report ranking VPEC second. The periods and market-share bases are not comparable, leaving the unqualified/current leadership claim unresolved.
