## Findings

EGAT's current company page says it has served more than 40 airlines worldwide. Its current scheduled-maintenance page lists A320/A330, B747, B767, and B787 families. The May 2025 issuer presentation says B747/B767/B777/B787 airframe-maintenance counts lead globally, and that A350/B737MAX capabilities and customer expansion are under development. It also reports 30 global major customers and eighth place in overall global airframe-maintenance capacity; those are different metrics and do not establish or refute the airline count or the B747-specific ranking.

Airways Magazine's 2025-10-23 facility report attributes to Cirium the finding that EGAT is the world's largest provider of C/heavy checks on four-engine aircraft, says that work is exclusively on Boeing 747 aircraft, and calls EGAT the largest 747 MRO provider worldwide. The report separately counts over 1,000 B747 heavy-check events, compared with 845 B767 and 750 B777 events. This is an industry-source claim tied to 2024 data, not an issuer statement.

The component claims are supported within these source and period bounds. The corrected wording keeps the airline count, maintenance-type list, B747 market position, and future A350/B737MAX capability development distinct. This is a candidate only; final adjudication remains required.
