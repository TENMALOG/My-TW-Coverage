# 重驗筆記 — 南六 / 6504:objective_ranking_or_market_share:a9d7ea309cd8da26

原始 claim：南六企業股份有限公司 (6504) 成立於 1972 年，總部位於台灣高雄，為亞洲水針 (Spunlace) 不織布供應龍頭暨全球前 40 大不織布製造商。

## 原子主張

1. `CONTRADICTED` — 南六企業股份有限公司成立於1972年。
   期間：Founding/establishment date
   說明：The issuer's own materials identify 1978 as the founding year; its audited financial report says it was approved for establishment in ROC year 67 (1978), not 1972.
   來源：[南六企業股份有限公司個體財務報告暨會計師查核報告（民國105年度及104年度）](https://www.nanliugroup.com/upload-files/finance/Financial_Statements/Parent_company_only_fincial_statements_105.pdf)；定位：Note 1, 公司沿革
   來源：[南六企業股份有限公司](https://www.nanliugroup.com/zh-CN)；定位：Taiwan headquarters contact details
2. `SUPPORTED` — 南六企業股份有限公司總部位於台灣高雄。
   期間：Issuer presentation identifies its operating headquarters in 2020; issuer website lists its Taiwan headquarters
   說明：The location is supported. The issuer presentation gives the operating-headquarters address in Yanchao, Kaohsiung, and the company website lists the Taiwan headquarters in Kaohsiung.
   來源：[南六企業股份有限公司 Nan Liu Enterprise CO.,LTD (issuer presentation)](https://www.twse.com.tw/staticFiles/news/event/ff8080817447bceb0174770e000e0179.pdf)；定位：Printed slides 11–12, industry ranking table and “Nonwovens Industry International Top 40 Companies”
   來源：[南六企業股份有限公司](https://www.nanliugroup.com/zh-CN)；定位：Taiwan headquarters contact details
3. `UNKNOWN` — 南六是亞洲水針（Spunlace）不織布供應龍頭。
   期間：Claim period unspecified; available ranking/leadership evidence is historical
   說明：A 2015 industry article reports historical global-leading annual spunlace output and identifies Nan Liu as Asia's first supplier of medical-grade waterjet fabric, with a 2013 market-share figure. This does not establish the unqualified, current claim about leadership in the broader Asian spunlace supply market.
   來源：[南六水針不織布產量 全球NO.1](https://www.tnet.org.tw/Article/Detail/17707)；定位：Article body, market-position paragraph
4. `UNKNOWN` — 南六是不織布製造商中的全球前40大。
   期間：The issuer presentation includes a 2016–2018 ranking table; no period is specified in the claim
   說明：The issuer presentation labels Nan Liu among the Nonwovens Industry International Top 40 Companies and includes historical company sales/rank data through 2018. This supports a historical snapshot but does not establish an unqualified current top-40 position.
   來源：[南六企業股份有限公司 Nan Liu Enterprise CO.,LTD (issuer presentation)](https://www.twse.com.tw/staticFiles/news/event/ff8080817447bceb0174770e000e0179.pdf)；定位：Printed slides 11–12, industry ranking table and “Nonwovens Industry International Top 40 Companies”

## 判定

Overall `PARTIAL`。

建議改寫：南六企業股份有限公司於1978年成立，營運總部位於台灣高雄市。

來源限制：
- The 1972 founding date conflicts with issuer materials and an audited issuer financial report, which give 1978.
- The spunlace-leadership evidence is secondary and historical; it concerns reported output and medical-grade waterjet fabric, not a verified current ranking of overall Asian suppliers.
- The global top-40 evidence is an issuer presentation's historical ranking snapshot, with the latest table year shown as 2018; it does not verify a current rank.

Focused source checks found direct issuer evidence for the founding year and headquarters, plus historical evidence for the two ranking claims. No evidence found here resolves the current period and precise market scope for the leadership claims, so no older ranking was treated as current.
