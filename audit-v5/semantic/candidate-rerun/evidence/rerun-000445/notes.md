# 重驗筆記 — 中華資安 / 7765:objective_ranking_or_market_share:5ea2c57aff387b29

原始 claim：[[中華電信]] — 全台最大 ISP 寬頻與 [[5G]] 網路骨幹，核心通路資源

## 原子主張

1. `UNKNOWN` — 中華電信是全台最大的 ISP 寬頻業者。
   期間：Original claim gives no period; current status not verified. Historical NCC data covers 2018–2020.
   說明：NCC material reports Chunghwa Telecom held the largest share of Taiwan's fixed-broadband retail market by account count in 2020 (59.77%). Chunghwa Telecom's 2024 disclosures report its own broadband and HiNet subscriber counts, but do not establish its rank against all providers for that period. Historical leadership does not verify the undated claim.
   來源：[「特定電信服務市場之市場顯著地位者認定及特別管制措施」公開諮詢文件](https://api.ncc.gov.tw/chncc/app/data/doc?aplistdn=undefined&detailNo=2&id=56&module=commonMessage8&preview=undefined&serno=48546_4195_news&type=s)；定位：Fixed communications network broadband retail service market; account-count market-share analysis and Figure 5
   來源：[Chunghwa Telecom 2024 Annual Report](https://www.cht.com.tw/home/cht/-/media/web/pdf/investors/annual-report/2024/2024_annual-report_cn_2.pdf)；定位：Distribution channels: physical service locations
2. `SUPPORTED` — 中華電信提供 5G 網路服務。
   期間：2024
   說明：Chunghwa Telecom's official 2024 annual report describes its 5G operations and reports nearly four million 5G subscribers. This supports provision of 5G service, not a ranking of a 5G backbone.
   來源：[Chunghwa Telecom 2024 Annual Report](https://www.cht.com.tw/home/cht/-/media/web/pdf/investors/annual-report/2024/2024_annual-report_cn_2.pdf)；定位：Distribution channels: physical service locations
3. `UNKNOWN` — 中華電信擁有全台最大的 5G 網路骨幹。
   期間：Original claim gives no period.
   說明：The wording does not define whether “5G network backbone” means core network, backhaul, base stations, coverage, capacity, or another measure. Issuer material describes network investment and claims a high number of mobile base stations, but those measures do not establish that its 5G backbone is the largest under a defined nationwide comparison.
   來源：[2024年第1季營運報告](https://www.cht.com.tw/zh-TW/home/cht/-/media/Web/PDF/Investors/Shareholder-Services/IR-Calendar/20240513/Chunghwa_Telecom_presentation_CH_1Q24.pdf)；定位：Mobile network advantages presentation
   來源：[Chunghwa Telecom 2024 Annual Report](https://www.cht.com.tw/home/cht/-/media/web/pdf/investors/annual-report/2024/2024_annual-report_cn_2.pdf)；定位：Distribution channels: physical service locations
4. `UNKNOWN` — 中華電信是中華資安的核心通路資源。
   期間：Original claim gives no period; available direct and secondary evidence is from 2018–2024.
   說明：There is evidence of Chunghwa Telecom operating a sizable physical service network and of collaboration with Chunghwa Security. A 2018 secondary report says the parent retained marketing and channel functions after the security subsidiary was established. The retrieved evidence does not establish that Chunghwa Telecom is a “core” channel resource for the subsidiary, or quantify the channel's role.
   來源：[Chunghwa Telecom 2024 Annual Report](https://www.cht.com.tw/home/cht/-/media/web/pdf/investors/annual-report/2024/2024_annual-report_cn_2.pdf)；定位：Distribution channels: physical service locations
   來源：[中華電信轉投資成立資安子公司 5年內達成上市櫃目標](https://www.ithome.com.tw/news/120758)；定位：Article discussion of the newly established Chunghwa Security subsidiary
   來源：[中華電信「防駭守門員」24小時守護上網安全……免費AI驗證服務「防詐隊長LINE 聊天機器人」全新上線！](https://www.cht.com.tw/zh-tw/home/cht/messages/2024/0513-1030)；定位：Announcement of a Chunghwa Telecom and Chunghwa Security collaboration

## 判定

Overall `PARTIAL`。

建議改寫：Chunghwa Telecom operated 5G services and reported 682 physical service locations at the end of 2024. NCC material establishes historical nationwide fixed-broadband account-share leadership for 2020, but the undated claims that Chunghwa Telecom remains Taiwan's largest ISP broadband provider, has the largest 5G network backbone, and is Chunghwa Security's core channel resource are not fully verified.

來源限制：
- The NCC broadband market-share evidence is historical and uses broadband account count as the denominator; it does not establish a current ranking.
- Chunghwa Telecom's subscriber and service-location figures are issuer disclosures, not independent comparisons with competitors.
- “5G network backbone” and “core channel resource” are not defined or quantified in the claim.
- The secondary channel-function report dates to 2018 and should not be treated as proof of current channel importance.

Stopped at the available tool-call iteration limit. The current nationwide broadband ranking, a defined comparative measure for 5G backbone size, and evidence that Chunghwa Telecom is a core channel resource for Chunghwa Security remain unresolved.
