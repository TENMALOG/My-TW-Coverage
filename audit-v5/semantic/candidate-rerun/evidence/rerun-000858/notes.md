# 重驗筆記 — 華航 / 2610:material_named_relationship:2e40073714d0a5be

原始 claim：[[台灣中油]] — 航空燃油獨家供應

## 原子主張

1. `SUPPORTED` — 台灣中油供應中華航空航空燃油。
   期間：2025; the report also covers 2024 supplier data.
   說明：China Airlines' annual report identifies aviation fuel supplied by CPC at Taiwan Taoyuan Airport. It also lists CPC as a major supplier. The supplier-spend table covers total net purchases, not aviation fuel alone.
   來源：[China Airlines 2025 Annual Report](https://calec.china-airlines.com/annual-report/annual_2025.pdf)；定位：Operational Highlights, §4.2.3, printed p. 129; §4.2.4, printed p. 130 (PDF pp. 131–132)
2. `CONTRADICTED` — 台灣中油是中華航空航空燃油的獨家供應商。
   期間：2025 operations; supplier table covers 2024 and 2025.
   說明：The issuer's report names both CPC and Formosa Petrochemical as suppliers of aviation fuel at Taoyuan, and says China Airlines also buys fuel from oil companies at destinations worldwide. This directly conflicts with exclusivity. The supplier-spend percentages are shares of total net purchases, not fuel-only shares.
   來源：[China Airlines 2025 Annual Report](https://calec.china-airlines.com/annual-report/annual_2025.pdf)；定位：Operational Highlights, §4.2.3, printed p. 129; §4.2.4, printed p. 130 (PDF pp. 131–132)

## 判定

Overall `PARTIAL`。

建議改寫：台灣中油是中華航空在桃園機場的航空燃油供應商之一，並非獨家供應商。

來源限制：
- The latest issuer annual report located covers 2025 and was listed by China Airlines on 2026-06-16.
- The annual report's supplier table reports shares of total net purchases, not aviation-fuel-only volumes or spend; the exclusivity finding rests on the report's explicit identification of multiple aviation-fuel suppliers.

The issuer's 2025 annual report directly supports CPC's supplier relationship and contradicts exclusivity; no unresolved atom remains that requires further searching.
