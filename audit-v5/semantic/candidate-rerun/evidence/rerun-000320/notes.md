# 重驗筆記 — 嘉聯益 / 6153:objective_ranking_or_market_share:aac6a63b7c86262a

原始 claim：[[Apple]](蘋果，最大且最重要的客戶)、各類非蘋智慧型手機與電腦品牌(如雙A、[[廣達]]、[[仁寶]]等)。

## 原子主張

1. `SUPPORTED` — Apple is a 嘉聯益 customer/supplier.
   期間：Apple supplier list for fiscal year 2022
   說明：Apple’s official supplier list names Career Technology (Mfg.) Company Limited and says the list represents 98% of Apple’s worldwide direct spend for materials, manufacturing, and assembly. This supports a supplier relationship, not a customer ranking.
   來源：[Apple Supplier List FY22](https://www.apple.com/tw/supplier-responsibility/pdf/Apple-Supplier-List.pdf)；定位：Supplier List, page 5
2. `UNKNOWN` — Apple is 嘉聯益’s largest and most important customer.
   期間：Unspecified in the claim
   說明：Evidence confirms Apple as a supplier but does not establish that it is the largest or most important customer. A 2023 issuer prospectus gives sales shares for the unnamed largest customer, so it cannot identify that customer as Apple.
   來源：[嘉聯益科技股份有限公司 Career Technology (Mfg.) Co., Ltd. 公開說明書](https://www.honsec.com.tw/uploads/underWriting/2132_4.pdf)；定位：Section on risks from sales concentration; first-largest-customer sales share
   來源：[Apple Supplier List FY22](https://www.apple.com/tw/supplier-responsibility/pdf/Apple-Supplier-List.pdf)；定位：Supplier List, page 5
3. `UNKNOWN` — 嘉聯益 serves various non-Apple smartphone and computer brands.
   期間：Unspecified in the claim
   說明：A 2019 secondary report mentions 嘉聯益’s entry into a non-Apple smartphone supply chain and names Huawei and OPPO, but this does not establish the broader smartphone-and-computer wording or its period.
   來源：[小三結盟拚軟硬板！瀚宇博德投37億入主嘉聯益，華新集團不惜代價劍指5G？](https://www.bnext.com.tw/article/54182/career-psa-hann-star-board-cooperate-5g-war)；定位：Article passage on 嘉聯益’s non-Apple smartphone supply chain and Huawei/OPPO
4. `UNKNOWN` — The named examples “雙A、廣達、仁寶” are 嘉聯益 customers.
   期間：Unspecified in the claim
   說明：The sources reviewed do not reliably establish these named entities as 嘉聯益 customers. Supplier status at Apple or a company’s product/application categories cannot substitute for evidence of these customer relationships.

## 判定

Overall `PARTIAL`。

建議改寫：嘉聯益列於Apple截至FY2022的供應商名單。2019年一篇次級報導提及嘉聯益進入非蘋手機供應鏈並點名華為、OPPO；現有證據未確認Apple是其最大或最重要客戶，也未確認「雙A、廣達、仁寶」為其客戶。

來源限制：
- Apple的供應商名單可確認供應商身分，但不揭露嘉聯益的Apple相關營收、採購額或客戶排名。
- 嘉聯益公開說明書披露了第一大客戶銷貨占比，但未指名該客戶。
- 2019年媒體報導只支持當時關於非蘋手機供應鏈的有限描述，不能外推至未標明的其他期間或電腦品牌。

已完成多輪聚焦搜尋；依工具迭代上限停止。Apple客戶排名及所列雙A、廣達、仁寶客戶關係仍缺少可核實證據。
