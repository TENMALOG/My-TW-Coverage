# 重驗筆記 — 雙鴻 / 3324:profitability_assertion:b0fb085043544429

原始 claim：(3) 均熱板 (VC) 技術 — 鞏固電競筆電與顯示卡散熱優勢，維持高毛利水平。

## 原子主張

1. `SUPPORTED` — VC design supports thermal cooling in gaming notebooks.
   期間：FY2024 report; gaming-notebook adoption described from 2023 onward.
   說明：The company reports that gaming-notebook makers have adopted VC cooling and lists high-performance gaming-notebook VC optimization among its R&D work. This supports VC use and design capability, but does not establish a comparative market advantage.
   來源：[Auras Technology 2024 Annual Report (Chinese)](https://www.auras.com.tw/CMSFS/Shareholders/FinancialAnnualChinese/ed4475a3-db60-455c-9ce9-93699a2fe601.pdf)；定位：PDF p. 2, FY2024 results; PDF p. 3, high-performance VC outlook
2. `SUPPORTED` — VC design supports thermal cooling in graphics cards.
   期間：Company disclosure dated 2025-05-19; FY2024 report also discusses 2025 high-end GPU adoption prospects.
   說明：Auras says it adopted VC with a dense fin structure for VGA cooling to improve performance stability and thermal conductivity. The annual report says higher-power RTX 50 cards are expected to raise VC penetration and ASP; these statements support cooling capability, not a quantified competitive ranking.
   來源：[Auras Technology, COMPUTEX 2025](https://www.auras.com.tw/News/NewsContent/14-8)；定位：Section 4, “Spec Migration”
   來源：[Auras Technology 2024 Annual Report (Chinese)](https://www.auras.com.tw/CMSFS/Shareholders/FinancialAnnualChinese/ed4475a3-db60-455c-9ce9-93699a2fe601.pdf)；定位：PDF p. 2, FY2024 results; PDF p. 3, high-performance VC outlook
3. `SUPPORTED` — Auras maintained a high company-wide gross margin over the reported FY2024–FY2025 period.
   期間：FY2024 and FY2025
   說明：The issuer described FY2024 consolidated gross margin as 26%, nearly 2 percentage points above FY2023 and a third consecutive annual high. A 2026-03-03 financial-statement report gives exact consolidated gross margins of 25.52% for FY2024 and 27.38% for FY2025. “High” is qualitative; no threshold is specified in the claim.
   來源：[Auras Technology 2024 Annual Report (Chinese)](https://www.auras.com.tw/CMSFS/Shareholders/FinancialAnnualChinese/ed4475a3-db60-455c-9ce9-93699a2fe601.pdf)；定位：PDF p. 2, FY2024 results; PDF p. 3, high-performance VC outlook
   來源：[MoneyDJ, Auras FY2025 comprehensive income statement](https://www.moneydj.com/kmdj/news/newsviewer.aspx?a=16342f7b-7c24-4992-bbbf-e41bbb725623)；定位：Income-statement table, revenue and gross-profit rows
4. `UNKNOWN` — VC technology specifically caused or maintained Auras’s high company-wide gross margin.
   期間：Unspecified in the claim; available margin evidence covers FY2024–FY2025.
   說明：The annual report says higher VC penetration may improve ASP and profitability structure, but does not quantify VC-related revenue, product-level gross margin, or its contribution to consolidated gross margin. Its FY2024 margin discussion attributes the improvement to overall product-mix optimization, including server liquid-cooling uptake/recovery and notebook specification upgrades. The specific VC-to-margin causal link is therefore unresolved.
   來源：[Auras Technology 2024 Annual Report (Chinese)](https://www.auras.com.tw/CMSFS/Shareholders/FinancialAnnualChinese/ed4475a3-db60-455c-9ce9-93699a2fe601.pdf)；定位：PDF p. 2, FY2024 results; PDF p. 3, high-performance VC outlook

## 判定

Overall `PARTIAL`。

建議改寫：雙鴻披露VC已用於電競筆電與顯示卡散熱，並稱相關滲透有助提升產品單價及獲利結構；其公司整體合併毛利率於FY2024及FY2025分別約為25.52%及27.38%。現有證據未能確認VC技術單獨維持該毛利率。

來源限制：
- “散熱優勢”是公司對其產品能力的描述，未有獨立市場排名或比較數據佐證。
- 毛利率數據是公司整體合併口徑，以合併營收為分母，不是電競筆電、VGA或VC產品線的毛利率。
- FY2024年報敘述將毛利率四捨五入為26%；MoneyDJ所列財報精確比例為25.52%。
- 官方FY2025年報下載連結在本次查詢中回傳404；FY2025毛利率數字引用MoneyDJ轉載的MOPS財報資料。

已完成針對公司年報、公司技術公告及財務披露的聚焦查核；剩餘不確定點是VC對公司整體毛利率的具體因果貢獻，公開資料未提供產品級毛利或歸因拆分。
