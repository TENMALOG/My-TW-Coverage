# 重驗筆記 — 太極 / 4934:profitability_assertion:2eee18322d02f40a

原始 claim：**美國市場：** 透過越南產能出口之主要獲利來源

## 原子主張

1. `SUPPORTED` — For 2020–2021, 太極 forecast that production was based in Vietnam and approximately 95% of its products would be sold to the higher-priced U.S. market, with the stated aim of increasing revenue and profit.
   期間：2020–2021 forecast
   說明：This supports a historical, forward-looking U.S.-market strategy—not proof that the forecast was achieved or that U.S. sales were a realized major profit source. The 95% denominator is products, not revenue or profit.
   來源：[太極能源科技股份有限公司, 2020 Investor Presentation](https://www.tainergy.com/storage/files/%E6%B3%95%E8%AA%AA%E6%9C%83/20200922%20%E5%A4%AA%E6%A5%B5-%E7%B0%A1%E5%A0%B1%E4%B8%AD%E6%96%87.pdf)；定位：Page 13
2. `CONTRADICTED` — 太極's own Vietnam production capacity continued to produce goods exported to the U.S. during FY2025.
   期間：FY2025
   說明：The FY2025 annual report describes handling equipment after the Vietnam factory closure and shifting resources to non-sensitive locations. This contradicts continued operation of the company's Vietnam production capacity in that period; it does not establish where any FY2025 U.S.-market goods were produced.
   來源：[太極能源科技股份有限公司, FY2025 Annual Report](https://financialfilings.com/filings/tainergy-4934/annual-report/2026/49882722/)；定位：Sales-region table and Vietnam factory closure discussion
3. `UNKNOWN` — Exports from Vietnam production capacity to the U.S. were a major realized source of 太極's profit.
   期間：Unspecified in original claim; reviewed evidence includes the 2020–2021 forecast and FY2025 disclosures
   說明：No reviewed disclosure attributes realized profit by destination and production origin, or defines a denominator for “major.” The FY2025 regional table reports sales revenue by destination (with U.S./Canada combined), not profit, and does not connect destination sales to Vietnam production.
   來源：[太極能源科技股份有限公司, 2020 Investor Presentation](https://www.tainergy.com/storage/files/%E6%B3%95%E8%AA%AA%E6%9C%83/20200922%20%E5%A4%AA%E6%A5%B5-%E7%B0%A1%E5%A0%B1%E4%B8%AD%E6%96%87.pdf)；定位：Page 13
   來源：[太極能源科技股份有限公司, FY2025 Annual Report](https://financialfilings.com/filings/tainergy-4934/annual-report/2026/49882722/)；定位：Sales-region table and Vietnam factory closure discussion
   來源：[太極能源科技股份有限公司, FY2020 Annual Report](https://www.tainergy.com/storage/files/%E8%82%A1%E6%9D%B1%E6%9C%83%E8%B3%87%E8%A8%8A/%E5%B9%B4%E5%A0%B1/%E4%B8%AD%E6%96%87/2020.pdf)；定位：Pages 59–60

## 判定

Overall `PARTIAL`。

建議改寫：太極曾預估2020–2021年約95%產品將利用越南產地銷往美國市場，以爭取營收及獲利；這是前瞻預估，並未證實實際獲利貢獻。

來源限制：
- The 2020–2021 evidence is a company forecast, not verified realized sales or earnings.
- The FY2025 sales-region figures are revenue, not profit; U.S. and Canada are combined, and the figures do not identify production origin.
- The FY2025 annual report text was accessed through a filing archive rather than a direct issuer-hosted URL.

Focused issuer and annual-report research is complete. The reviewed disclosures do not provide realized profit attribution by both destination and production origin, so that atom remains UNKNOWN.
