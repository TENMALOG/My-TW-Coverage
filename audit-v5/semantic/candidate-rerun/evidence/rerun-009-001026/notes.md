## 判定

Overall `PARTIAL`。

建議改寫：A secondary report published on January 23, 2017 identified Garmin as one of 閎暉’s wearable-device customers. The available evidence does not establish the specific Garmin parts supplied, a close relationship, or primary-supplier status.

來源限制：
- The direct Garmin relationship evidence found is secondary and dates to 2017; it should not be treated as proof of a current relationship.
- 閎暉’s FY2025 official disclosure discusses wearable products but does not name Garmin or disclose the claimed customer-specific parts or supplier ranking.
- The 2017 article’s mention of Garmin as a wearable customer and its separate discussion of smartwatch straps do not by themselves prove that the straps were supplied to Garmin.

Focused searches and review of the issuer’s FY2025 disclosure and relevant 2017 reporting found evidence for historical Garmin customer status only; the product-specific, relationship-quality, and primary-supplier atoms remain unresolved.
