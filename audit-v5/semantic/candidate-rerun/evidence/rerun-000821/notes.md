## 判定

Overall `PARTIAL`。

建議改寫：BBI's online ordering is linked to warehouse availability/location and shipping features. BBI self-describes as North America's largest master distributor, but the broader “largest fastener distribution platform” ranking and integration of procurement with those functions are not established by the evidence reviewed.

來源限制：
- The ranking evidence consists of company self-descriptions, not an independent market comparison.
- “Largest master distributor,” “largest U.S. fastener import distribution channel,” and “largest North American fastener distribution platform” are not interchangeable market definitions.
- BBI's official materials document online-order, warehouse, and shipping linkages but do not resolve integration of procurement into that same platform.
- Some procurement/sourcing evidence is historical; it does not establish the current platform configuration.

Completed six focused search turns. Official sources support the warehouse/order/shipping linkage, but the broader ranking and current procurement integration remain unresolved; those atoms are retained as UNKNOWN.
