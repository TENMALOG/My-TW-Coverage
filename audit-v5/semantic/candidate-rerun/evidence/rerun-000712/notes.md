## 判定

Overall `PARTIAL`。

建議改寫：MoneyDJ reported Stanley Black & Decker as 巨庭’s largest customer in 2024 and SawStop as its second-largest customer; 巨庭’s official site identifies DEWALT as a Stanley Black & Decker brand. The claim that 巨庭 supplied TTI (RYOBI) remains unverified.

來源限制：
- The customer ranking and SawStop relationship are supported by secondary reporting, not a primary issuer customer schedule found in this research.
- The 2024 report does not state the denominator or units for its customer-share figures. Its ranking is period-bound and does not establish the largest customer outside 2024.
- The TTS Tooltechnic Systems supplier award must not be treated as evidence of a relationship with TTI.

Focused searches found no reliable evidence for 巨庭 supplying TTI. The available sources distinguish the TTS award from TTI and do not resolve the missing customer relationship, so that atom remains UNKNOWN.
