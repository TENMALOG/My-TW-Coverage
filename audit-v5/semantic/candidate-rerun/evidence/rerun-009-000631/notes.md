## 原子主張

1. `UNKNOWN` — 全球前20大半導體廠中近九成為穎崴客戶。
   期間：Mirror Media reported this as current in an article dated 2026-07-14; the measurement period is not specified.
   說明：Mirror Media and a 104 employer profile report the near-90% figure, but neither identifies the top-20 list, ranking basis or period, or what qualifies a company as a customer. The implied denominator is 20 companies, but it cannot be independently bound to a defined market or ranking. The issuer's retrieved disclosures do not substantiate this customer statistic.
   來源：[穎崴大擴產1／輝達、超微都靠穎崴 揭測試探針廠成晶片出廠關鍵原因](https://www.mirrormedia.mg/story/20260714fin003)；定位：Paragraph beginning「本刊調查，目前全球前20大半導體廠中」
   來源：[穎崴科技股份有限公司｜104人力銀行公司介紹](https://www.104.com.tw/company/61enk4w)；定位：公司簡介 paragraph beginning「測試領導廠商穎崴創立於2001年」
2. `SUPPORTED` — 穎崴供應用於半導體及高階運算測試的測試介面產品，可視為AI供應鏈中的「賣鏟子」供應商。
   期間：Issuer disclosure dated 2025-06-26; AI/HPC shipment mix reported as of May 2025.
   說明：The issuer describes its test sockets and vertical probe cards, including solutions for AI/HPC applications. Its reported 42% figure is a share of shipments, not revenue.「賣鏟子」is a metaphor, not a separately measured industry category.
   來源：[穎崴續攻先進製程 7奈米以下營收比重近九成](https://www.winwayglobal.com/zh-TW/news-detail/tgN1Sz7yjF0n7ap2)；定位：Company press release; paragraphs beginning「穎崴董事長王嘉煌表示」and「穎崴長期投入高階測試介面開發」

   期間：Fiscal year 2025; issuer annual report published 2026-04-25.
   說明：The issuer attributes 2025 performance to strong AI, HPC, and ASIC momentum, supporting a business benefit from AI-related demand. This does not establish that AI alone caused the results or quantify its individual contribution.「軍備競賽」is rhetorical framing rather than a defined market measure.
   來源：[2025 Annual Report](https://www.winwayglobal.com/dl/file/D1nEkzuLhsqH2IyC)；定位：Business plan implementation results
   來源：[穎崴科技2024年第2季財報新聞稿](https://www.winwayglobal.com/zh-TW/news-detail/hy8TCaR7g0aA0gzS)；定位：Paragraph beginning「在AI、HPC需求強勁下」

## 判定

Overall `PARTIAL`。

建議改寫：穎崴供應半導體測試介面產品，並披露AI/HPC需求及相關出貨支持其業務；其「全球前20大半導體廠近九成為客戶」的說法缺少可核對的排名名單、排名期間與客戶口徑，尚無法驗證。

來源限制：
- 「全球前20大半導體廠」沒有指定排名機構、排名年份或企業範圍；來源未列出20家名單。
- 公司披露的42%是截至2025年5月的AI/HPC應用出貨占比，不能替代營收占比或客戶數驗證。
- 「賣鏟子」及「AI軍備競賽」屬比喻性描述；可驗證的部分是公司產品與AI/HPC需求、業務表現的關聯。

已查閱 issuer disclosures、年報與報導及雇主公司簡介；AI/HPC業務關聯有支持，但未找到能定義並核對「全球前20大」名單、期間與客戶口徑的資料，因此該原子主張維持UNKNOWN並停止研究。
