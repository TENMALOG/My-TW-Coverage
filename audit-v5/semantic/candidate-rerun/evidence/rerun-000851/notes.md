# 重驗筆記 — 晟田 / 4541:material_named_relationship:df473dfcdc3e30d8

   說明：The issuer lists components for beverage, food, and pharmaceutical aseptic filling/packaging and capping equipment. This supports the product category, not its revenue share.

## 判定

Overall `PARTIAL`。

建議改寫：晟田年報列有食品飲料無菌充填、包裝及鎖蓋設備精密零組件；櫃買中心2013年公司資料記載其曾與三菱重工合作供應相關設備零組件。食品包裝設備營收占比約10%及其他客戶未獲單獨證實。

來源限制：
- The approximately 10% figure is not verified for food-packaging equipment alone. The closest primary-source figures are for a broader combined category covering food machinery, precision machinery, and other parts processing.
- The Mitsubishi relationship is supported historically; these sources do not confirm that it continues today.
- The additional-customer wording remains unverified.

Focused searches of issuer annual reports, the issuer website, and TPEx material established the product category and historical Mitsubishi relationship. The available primary disclosures do not isolate the claimed revenue share or identify additional customers, so those atoms remain UNKNOWN.
