# 重驗筆記 — 東科-KY / 5225:objective_ranking_or_market_share:534c2c4cfb9c89c8

原始 claim：1. **Sound Bar (聲霸)**: 全球市佔率極高，為主要營收成長動能。

## 原子主張

1. `UNKNOWN` — Sound Bar (聲霸) has an extremely high global market share.
   期間：Claim period unspecified; located secondary evidence concerns 2021–2022.
   說明：No evidence found that defines 東科-KY’s global Sound Bar market share by denominator or unit. MoneyDJ described it as the world’s largest Soundbar ODM and reported Soundbar revenue as 70% of the company’s Q3 2022 revenue and about 51% for FY2021. Those are an ODM ranking and company revenue-mix figures, not 東科-KY’s share of the global Sound Bar market. The issuer’s 2024 presentation reports revenue by broader product categories, not global market share.
   來源：[東科-KY：電視薄型化成Soundbar銷售關鍵 | MoneyDJ理財網 | LINE TODAY](https://today.line.me/tw/v3/article/8nwmy5q)；定位：Article text on Soundbar ODM ranking and revenue proportions
   來源：[東科控股股份有限公司 2024年第三季法人說明會簡報](https://www.eastech.com/sites/default/files/institutional-investors/2024-11/20241111%20-%20%E6%B3%95%E8%AA%AA%E6%9C%83%E7%B0%A1%E5%A0%B1%E6%AA%94_%E4%B8%AD%E6%96%87%E7%89%88.pdf)；定位：產品種類結構 tables
2. `SUPPORTED` — Sound Bar is a major revenue-growth driver for 東科-KY.
   期間：2022, including the first 11 months of 2022.
   說明：CNA reported that Soundbar sales volume increased and that strong Soundbar sales benefited 東科-KY as its cumulative revenue through November 2022 rose 40% year over year. This supports a qualitative growth-driver claim for that period, but does not quantify Sound Bar’s share of incremental revenue or establish that it remains a major growth driver in other periods. The claim itself gives no period.
   來源：[OTT浪潮推升聲霸銷售 東科-KY今年營運拚新高](https://www.cna.com.tw/news/afe/202212080294.aspx)；定位：Opening paragraphs and company operating outlook
   來源：[東科控股股份有限公司 2024年度年報](https://www.eastech.com/sites/default/files/2025-05/2024zhongwennianbao_-_suo_0.pdf)；定位：營業報告書: 2024年度營業計畫實施成果 and 2025年營業計畫概要

## 判定

Overall `PARTIAL`。

建議改寫：2022年，東科-KY受惠Soundbar銷量放量而營運轉強；MoneyDJ曾稱其為全球最大Soundbar ODM廠，但現有證據未確認其全球Sound Bar市佔率、分母或單位。

來源限制：
- Soundbar占東科-KY自身營收的比例，不等於東科-KY在全球Sound Bar市場的市佔率。
- 全球最大Soundbar ODM廠是市場排名描述，不提供市佔率數值或明確市場分母。
- 2022年營收成長動能的報導不能直接證明該產品在未註明期間仍是主要成長動能；官方產品營收分類也未單列Sound Bar。

已查閱公司年報與法人說明會資料及相關財經媒體報導；尚未找到界定全球Sound Bar市場分母、單位及東科-KY市佔率的可靠資料，因此該原子主張維持UNKNOWN。於達到8輪搜尋上限前停止。
