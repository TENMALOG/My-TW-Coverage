## 判定

Overall `PARTIAL`。

建議改寫：鈺鎧是車用電子磁性被動元件供應商；公司資料列出的應用包括頭燈/尾燈、胎壓偵測及儀表板/ADAS。歷史資料支持其與歐系車用電子供應鏈合作及曾進入美國車用電子市場，但目前可得資料未證實特定 Tier 1 關係、直接供貨層級或當前客戶供貨狀況。

來源限制：
- The 2026 application evidence comes from an issuer-authored investor presentation reproduced on a third-party database, not an issuer-hosted source.
- That database labels the event as 2026Q3, while the presentation cover identifies it as a 2026 second-quarter briefing; slide 26 is used only for its stated application list.
- Older market-relationship evidence does not establish present-day continuity, named customers, shipment volumes, or direct-versus-indirect supply.
- The sources describe component applications, not鈺鎧 as a vehicle-lamp or complete ADAS-system manufacturer.

Focused searches found evidence for the listed automotive applications and historical European/U.S. automotive-electronics market relationships, but did not resolve a specific Tier 1 relationship or direct supply tier. Those unresolved scope points remain UNKNOWN.
