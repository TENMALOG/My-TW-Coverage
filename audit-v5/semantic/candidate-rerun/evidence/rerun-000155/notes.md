## 判定

Overall `PARTIAL`。

建議改寫：Netronix reports e-reader software, user-interface, and manufacturing capabilities and develops e-paper products and applications. DIGITIMES has described it as the world's largest e-reader ODM, but the available evidence does not establish the ranking's period, peer set, or measurement basis.

來源限制：
- The global-largest ranking appears in a secondary DIGITIMES article; the ranking's source methodology, metric, denominator, and measurement period were not located.
- The official capability and expertise statements are issuer disclosures and do not constitute independent comparative benchmarking.
- The original claim has no stated as-of date.

Focused research found official issuer evidence supporting the capability and e-paper application atoms, but no disclosed basis sufficient to verify the global-largest ranking. That atom remains UNKNOWN.
