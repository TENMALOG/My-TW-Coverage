- The undated SAS Group Profile contains a broad largest-supplier claim but also states that the semiconductor business was carved out to GlobalWafers in 2011; it further lists SAS products and services as solar ingots/wafers. Treat its broad ranking statement as temporally and organizationally ambiguous.

Focused official-source research found historical support for the spun-off subsidiary but no unambiguous current, issuer-specific 3–12-inch product scope or Taiwan ranking with a defined denominator. Those atoms remain UNKNOWN.
