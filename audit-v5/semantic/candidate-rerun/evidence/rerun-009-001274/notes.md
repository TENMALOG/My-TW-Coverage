## 判定

Overall `CANDIDATE_ACCEPTED`。

建議改寫：CPC (台灣中油) supplied 欣雄天然氣 and was described as Taiwan’s exclusive domestic natural-gas supplier in the issuer’s FY2024 annual report; a Legislative Yuan report corroborates the national supplier position through July 2025.

來源限制：
- The issuer’s newer FY2025 annual report was checked but does not repeat the CPC supplier/exclusivity statement; that omission is not evidence of a change.
- The issuer annual report’s 96.48% figure is CPC’s share of total net purchases, not a natural-gas-only purchase denominator. The exclusivity finding rests on the report’s separate express statement.

Focused research limit reached. The full wording is explicitly supported for FY2024, with official corroboration through July 2025; no later issuer disclosure directly restating exclusivity was located.
