# 重驗筆記 — 環拓科技 / 7507:objective_ranking_or_market_share:bfc75b334da69cf9

原始 claim：環拓科技股份有限公司 (7507，ENRESTEC) 為全球唯一成功商業化廢輪胎化學熱裂解之環保科技公司。

## 原子主張

1. `SUPPORTED` — 環拓科技股份有限公司 is the company identified as ENRESTEC and ticker 7507.
   期間：Current identification, checked 2026-10-07
   說明：The issuer’s official site identifies the company as Enrestec and lists 7507 as its TPEx ticker.
   來源：[Enrestec Inc. – Waste Tire Pyrolysis · rCB · TPO · Equipment Sales](https://www.enrestec.com.tw/en/)；定位：“The world's first waste tire pyrolysis plant in 24-hour commercial operation” and company timeline
2. `SUPPORTED` — 環拓科技 is an environmental-technology company.
   期間：Current company description, checked 2026-10-07
   說明：The issuer describes its work as environmental solutions and waste-resource recovery; this supports the broad descriptive label, not an independent industry classification.
   來源：[About Us – Enrestec](https://www.enrestec.com.tw/en/about/)；定位：About Us; company description and pyrolysis resource-recovery activities
3. `SUPPORTED` — 環拓科技 has successfully commercialized waste-tire chemical/thermal pyrolysis.
   期間：At least 2013 onward, according to issuer disclosure
   說明：The issuer reports opening a 24-hour commercial waste-tire pyrolysis plant in Taiwan in 2013 and describes commercial production of recovered carbon black, pyrolysis oil, and steel.
   來源：[Enrestec Inc. – Waste Tire Pyrolysis · rCB · TPO · Equipment Sales](https://www.enrestec.com.tw/en/)；定位：“The world's first waste tire pyrolysis plant in 24-hour commercial operation” and company timeline
4. `CONTRADICTED` — 環拓科技 is the only company globally to have successfully commercialized waste-tire chemical/thermal pyrolysis.
   期間：At least 2015 onward; the original claim gives no as-of date
   說明：ReOil in Poland is a counterexample: a 2016 Messer publication reports that its waste-tire pyrolysis plant had operated since 2015 and produced carbon black and pyrolysis oil. ReOil’s own history reports plant startup during 2013–2016 and later commercial progress, including profitability and long-term product contracts. This contradicts global exclusivity under the ordinary meaning of commercialized waste-tire pyrolysis.
   來源：[Second life for used tyres, Gases for Life, Issue 01 | June 2016](https://corporate.messergroup.com/documents/20127/4893390/Gases%2Bfor%2BLife%2Bno.%2B18%2B-%2BFollowing%2Bnature%E2%80%99s%2Bexample.pdf/e8bab825-40a0-6a31-e6a9-b7533ac110aa?t=1597676191210)；定位：Article “Second life for used tyres”; ReOil plant in Myślenice, Poland
   來源：[About us | reoil.pl](https://reoil.pl/en/about-us/)；定位：Development / History timeline

## 判定

Overall `PARTIAL`。

建議改寫：環拓科技（7507）已商業化廢輪胎熱裂解；但現有證據不支持其為全球唯一，且有波蘭 ReOil 廢輪胎熱裂解商業營運的反例。

來源限制：
- 環拓與 ReOil 的公司網站屬於當事公司披露；ReOil 的商業營運另有 Messer 2016 年刊物佐證。
- 原文未指定「成功商業化」的門檻或評估日期；全球唯一性依通常含義檢核，未改成特定連續式製程或其他更窄範圍。
