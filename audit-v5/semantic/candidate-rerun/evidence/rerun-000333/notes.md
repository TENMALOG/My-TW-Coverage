## 判定

Overall `PARTIAL`。

建議改寫：WAFERLOCK’s issuer disclosures describe in-house development of IoT smart-lock and digital access-control products for settings including offices, hotels, and residential communities. A 2025 pre-listing report put IoT smart-product revenue at about 25%, without specifying the exact measurement period. Media reported increasing Europe-region revenue share and improving consolidated gross margin, but the B2B share above 50% lacks a defined market denominator, and the evidence does not establish that a higher Europe export share caused the margin improvement.

來源限制：
- The B2B share above 50% is reported by secondary media without a specified geography, product-market definition, denominator, measurement method, or period.
- A separate report gives approximately 50% for Taiwan’s new-build electronic-lock market; that narrower metric is not interchangeable with overall B2B market share.
- The approximately 25% IoT revenue share is a secondary report from the 2025 pre-listing period, with no precise reporting window stated.
- Reported Europe-region revenue shares are not identified as a percentage of exports; the export-only denominator is not disclosed in the cited report.
- The cited margin comparison is secondary reporting. It supports an increase in consolidated margin but not a Europe-export-specific causal effect.

Focused searches covered issuer annual-report disclosures, the official WAFERLOCK brand description, a Ministry of Economic Affairs profile, and contemporaneous financial-media reports. Further evidence located did not resolve the B2B denominator or the Europe-export causal link, so those atoms remain UNKNOWN.
