# 重驗筆記 — 台玻 / 1802:objective_ranking_or_market_share:75e14cf20248f928

原始 claim：台玻 (Taiwan Glass Ind. Corp.) 成立於 1964 年，總部位於台灣[[台北]]，為國內最大、全球名列前茅的玻璃製造集團。

## 原子主張

1. `SUPPORTED` — 台玻（Taiwan Glass Ind. Corp.）成立於1964年。
   期間：Founded in 1964
   說明：The issuer’s official website states that Taiwan Glass was founded in 1964. The claim gives only the year, which the source supports.
   來源：[TAIWANGLASS—Official website](https://taiwanglass.com/?langeno=en)；定位：Homepage statement: “Taiwan Glass was founded in 1964.”

   期間：Location stated in the financial statements for the year ended 2023-12-31
   說明：The issuer’s filing identifies Taipei City as the company’s registered place and principal operating location. This supports the stated Taiwan/Taipei location.
   來源：[台灣玻璃工業股份有限公司 2024 股東會年報](https://www.taiwanglass.com/tc/pdf/2024%E8%82%A1%E6%9D%B1%E6%9C%83%E5%B9%B4%E5%A0%B1.pdf)；定位：個體財務報表附註一「公司沿革」：註冊地及主要營運據點位於臺北市南京東路三段261號11樓。

   期間：Not specified in the claim; a secondary source makes a similar statement on 2026-03-04

   來源：[台玻拚市占翻倍 中長線仍需留意這幾項 - 理財周刊](https://www.moneyweekly.com.tw/ArticleData/Info/Article/211509)；定位：Opening paragraph: describes the company as Taiwan’s largest glass manufacturer.

An undated trade-platform profile asserts a top-five position but provides no ranking basis and is not reliable enough to resolve the broad claim.
   來源：[News Detail - TAIWANGLASS](https://www.taiwanglass.com/news_detail.php?id=2449&langeno=en&sid=334)；定位：Company description: fiberglass and fiberglass-fabric capacity occupies a significant position in the global market.

## 判定

Overall `PARTIAL`。

建議改寫：台玻（Taiwan Glass Ind. Corp.）成立於1964年；其登記地及主要營運據點位於台灣台北市。

來源限制：
- The issuer’s 1964 statement supports the year only, not a specific founding day.
- The domestic-largest statement found in secondary coverage lacks a ranking measure and comparison denominator.
- The available global-ranking references are either limited to particular product capacities or undated and methodologically unspecified; neither verifies the broad group-level claim.

Stopped after eight focused research turns. The founding year and Taipei location were supported, but the domestic and global ranking atoms remain unresolved because their comparison scope, period, and measurement basis are unspecified or unsupported.
