## 判定

Overall `PARTIAL`。

建議改寫：鴻華先進曾被報導為MIH聯盟創始會員。鴻海稱2022年交付的Model T超過65%零組件由MIH聯盟成員與台灣供應商合計供應；資料未披露台灣供應商單獨佔比逾50%。

來源限制：
- MIH membership evidence supports Foxtron’s historical founding-member status; it does not verify its current membership status.
- The Model T percentage is a combined sourcing figure for MIH Alliance members and Taiwanese suppliers, not a standalone Taiwan-supplier percentage.
- No reliable source found that defines the denominator, measurement basis, or period for the claimed Taiwan-supplier share.

Seven focused search turns found evidence for the historical founding-member relationship and a related but non-equivalent Model T sourcing figure. The Taiwan-supplier percentage remains unresolved because its scope, period, and denominator are unspecified.
