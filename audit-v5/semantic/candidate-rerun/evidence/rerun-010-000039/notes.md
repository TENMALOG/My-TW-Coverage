# 重驗筆記 — 江申 / 1525:objective_ranking_or_market_share:67775914202401b9

原始 claim：[[中華汽車]]、[[國瑞汽車]] — 車架 (市佔 100%)

## 原子主張

1. `SUPPORTED` — 江申 began supplying large-truck and bus frames to 國瑞汽車 and 中華汽車.
   期間：Beginning May 1984; the source does not state whether supply continues today.
   說明：江申’s official company history records that it began supplying large-truck and bus frames to both companies in May 1984. This supports the historical supplier/product relationship, not necessarily a current one.
   來源：[江申工業股份有限公司 — Company History](https://www.kian-shen.com/tw/Web/page/51)；定位：Company History, entry dated 1984年05月
2. `UNKNOWN` — 江申’s frame market share was 100%.
   說明：A secondary company profile attributes 100% share to frames for domestically manufactured trucks above 3.5 tonnes (large trucks and buses), but gives no measurement period or explicit denominator/calculation basis. 江申’s 2024 sustainability report describes frames as an oligopoly-market product but does not state a 100% share. The unqualified original wording is therefore not fully verified.
   來源：[江申工業股份有限公司-MoneyDJ理財網](https://www.moneydj.com/kmdj/wiki/wikiviewer.aspx?keyid=ba345a19-2ea3-4cef-b535-2064da9f7be4)；定位：Company profile, section「公司簡介」
   來源：[江申工業永續報告書（2024）](https://www.kian-shen.com/product_image/images/users/KSC-B320/%E6%B0%B8%E7%BA%8C%E5%A0%B1%E5%91%8A%E6%9B%B8/2024%E5%B9%B4%20%E6%B0%B8%E7%BA%8C%E5%A0%B1%E5%91%8A%E6%9B%B8.pdf)；定位：Chapter 3, description of principal products and market position

## 判定

Overall `PARTIAL`。

建議改寫：江申官方沿革記載，該公司於1984年5月開始供應國瑞汽車與中華汽車大貨車及巴士車架。二手公司資料稱，江申在3.5噸以上國產貨車（大貨車、巴士）車架的市佔率達100%；該市佔率的期間與分母尚未核實。

來源限制：
- The official issuer history establishes a supply relationship beginning in 1984, but does not establish that the relationship remains current.
- The 100% figure is found in secondary company-profile material, not in the reviewed issuer sustainability report; its period and denominator are unspecified.
- The narrower market scope in the secondary source should not be broadened to all vehicle frames or treated as a current measured share.

Focused searches located official issuer history and the 2024 sustainability report, plus a secondary source for the narrower 100% claim. The share’s measurement period and denominator remain unresolved.
