## 判定

Overall `PARTIAL`。

建議改寫：媒體報導聯友金屬為台積電的合格鎢供應商；其是否為台灣唯一合格供應商，以及供應產品、資格範圍與有效期間，尚未獲獨立資料確認。

來源限制：
- The supplier and exclusivity assertions are reported by secondary media; the reviewed TSMC disclosure does not confirm the relationship or identify a supplier.
- The issuer's exchange-hosted presentation lists tungsten products including sodium tungstate, but does not specify what product the reported TSMC supply concerns.
- No denominator, qualification criteria, geographic boundary beyond the phrase「在台」, or effective period was found for the word「唯一」.

Focused searches and checks of issuer, exchange-hosted, media, and TSMC materials found secondary reporting of the assertion but no verifiable supplier record or defined basis for exclusivity. The exclusivity atom remains UNKNOWN.
