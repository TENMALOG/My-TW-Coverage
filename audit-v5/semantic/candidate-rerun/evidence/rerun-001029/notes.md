## 判定

Overall `PARTIAL`。

建議改寫：雲嘉南科技股份有限公司（股票代號3191）前身為和進電子股份有限公司；公司名稱於2023年7月17日登記變更，相關新股於2024年2月21日開始櫃檯買賣。

來源限制：
- The official registry establishes the legal name-change date; the TPEx notice describes the subsequent stock-replacement and trading schedule.
- The TPEx notice was issued in 2024, but its issuance date should not be conflated with the 2023 registry date of the corporate name change.

Official registry and TPEx records resolve the identity and timing distinction; no material atom remains unresolved.
