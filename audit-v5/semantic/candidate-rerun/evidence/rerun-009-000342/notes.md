## 判定

Overall `PARTIAL`。

建議改寫：ITH Corporation（奕力-KY，6962）由奕力科技與晨星科技（MStar）旗下觸控 IC 業務在聯發科體系中整合而來，從事顯示驅動 IC 與觸控 IC 相關業務。公司曾披露其 TDDI 市佔率於 2020 年全球排名第二；此歷史排名不等同於目前排名。

來源限制：
- The origin claim names 聯詠 (Novatek), but the reviewed historical accounts identify 晨星科技 (MStar); those entities should not be conflated.
- The second-place ranking is supported as a historical company-reported claim. The sources do not provide market share percentages, the market denominator, measurement basis, or methodology, and do not establish that the ranking remains current.

Focused searches found issuer, exchange, interview, and partner-release evidence sufficient to resolve the origin discrepancy and historical ranking. The remaining current-ranking and market-methodology details were not established.
