## 判定

Overall `PARTIAL`。

建議改寫：Contemporaneous reports from 2018–2020 stated that 同泰 supplied wireless-charging soft boards for Samsung Galaxy S10/S20-era phones; a December 2018 report described it as Samsung's No. 1 wireless-charging-board supplier for the S10 order. These reports do not establish a current supplier relationship or a broader ranking among Korean suppliers.

來源限制：
- The located customer-specific evidence is secondary reporting, not an official issuer disclosure or Samsung filing.
- Historical model-specific reports cannot establish that the relationship continues at the time of this review.
- The original wording does not state a period, and “major Korean suppliers” lacks a defined market and ranking denominator.

After focused searches of company-source material and Taiwanese financial/news sources, historical model-specific evidence was located, but no current official confirmation or evidence resolving the broader supplier-ranking scope was found. Further searches were stopped with those scope and period questions unresolved.
