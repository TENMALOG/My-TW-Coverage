來源限制：
- The detailed description of the center's purpose comes from the issuer's own corporate profile, which has no publication date shown.
- Nikkei independently supports the 1992 joint establishment and the Nike-specific first-in-Asia description, but does not establish an all-company Asia-wide first.
- The original wording can be read as an absolute Asia-wide ranking; that broader scope remains unverified.

Focused searches found issuer and Nikkei support for Nike's first Asia-based center and issuer support for the stated R&D fields. No evidence established an all-company Asia-wide first, so that broader ranking remains UNKNOWN; further searches were unlikely to resolve it without a defined comparison universe or contemporaneous records.
