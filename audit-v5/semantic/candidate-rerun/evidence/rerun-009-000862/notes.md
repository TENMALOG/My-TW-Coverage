## 判定

Overall `PARTIAL`。

建議改寫：森鉅2017年3月的投資人簡報將 Wabash National、Great Dane、Utility Trailer 和 Stoughton Trailers 列於現有客戶相關頁面；森鉅2023年12月31日財報列有 Vanguard National Trailer Corp. 的銷貨應收帳款。現有證據不足以確認這五家公司是北美五大拖車製造商，或共同構成森鉅的主要客戶群。

來源限制：
- The named-customer evidence is not contemporaneous: the issuer presentation is dated March 2017, while the Vanguard receivable is reported at December 31, 2023.
- The 2017 presentation’s trailer-unit sales and market-share figures are not sales to 森鉅; the table does not establish customer revenue concentration.
- The 2025 financial report discloses only anonymized identities for customers above its 10%-of-consolidated-net-revenue threshold, so it cannot be used to map major-customer status to the five named companies.
- No source located establishes the exact North American market definition, ranking period, or denominator for the claimed group of five.

Focused research found historical issuer evidence for four named customer relationships and a 2023 receivable for Vanguard, but not evidence resolving the North American top-five designation or the collective main-customer assertion. The latest located financial report anonymizes major-customer identities; unresolved scope and customer-concentration atoms remain UNKNOWN.
