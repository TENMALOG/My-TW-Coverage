## 判定

Overall `PARTIAL`。

建議改寫：Secondary reporting identifies NTN, Dana, and AAM among 江興鍛’s customers and describes its products as transmission-system forged parts. NTN was reported as the largest customer for 2014, while a 2020 report identified China Honda as the largest customer in 2019; the largest-customer claim therefore requires a specified period.

來源限制：
- Search results provided secondary-source excerpts; no official issuer disclosure confirming these customer names or the ranking was located.
- The customer and product statements lack a clearly established effective period in the available excerpts.
- The reported 2014 figure is NTN’s share of 江興鍛 revenue, not a market-share measurement.

Research stopped after reaching the tool-call limit. The remaining uncertainty is the period-specific largest-customer ranking and whether the named customer relationships remain current.
