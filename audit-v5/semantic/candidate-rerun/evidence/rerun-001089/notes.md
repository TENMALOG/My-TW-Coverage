# 重驗筆記 — GIS-KY / 6456:material_named_relationship:251573f509b34a00

原始 claim：[[Apple]](iPad及MacBook觸控/顯示模組之核心供應商)、三星及各大[[AR]]/[[VR]]頭戴裝置品牌。

## 原子主張

1. `SUPPORTED` — GIS-KY is a core supplier of touch/display modules for Apple's iPad and MacBook.
   期間：Documented for 2024; continued customer-specific status after that period is not established here.
   說明：A KGI Asia supply-chain report dated 2024-07-05 lists GIS under touch-panel modules for both iPad and MacBook and gives 2024F sales weighting of 80+. MoneyDJ separately reported GIS as an important supplier of iPad touch modules and MacBook LCD modules in 2020. These are secondary sources; GIS's 2025 annual report discusses leading-brand supply relationships generically and does not name Apple.
   來源：[Apple supply chain](https://www.kgi.com.hk/zh-cn/-/media/files/kgishk/research-reports/tw-reports/2024/01/apple-supply-chain_05072024.pdf)；定位：Page 5, Table 12, row “Touch panel module,” GIS (6456 TT); iPad and MacBook columns and 2024F sales-weighting column.
   來源：[GIS-KY受惠蘋果Q4平穩；京東方訂單待放量](https://www.moneydj.com/KMDJ/News/NewsViewer.aspx?a=fa361fe0-40d2-45d7-ab25-1788d8425270)；定位：Paragraph 47.
   來源：[General Interface Solution (GIS) Holding Limited — Annual Report 2025](https://financialfilings.com/filings/gis-6456/annual-report/2026/47935749/)；定位：Industry overview, page 87; marketing strategy, lines 2674–2679.
2. `SUPPORTED` — Samsung is a GIS-KY customer or named product relationship.
   期間：Historical reporting from 2020 and 2022.
   說明：Secondary reporting identifies Samsung as one of GIS's smartphone-brand customers and reports GIS-developed fingerprint modules for Samsung flagship phones. The evidence does not establish the current status or show that Samsung is a customer for the same iPad/MacBook module categories.
   來源：[GIS-KY受惠蘋果Q4平穩；京東方訂單待放量](https://www.moneydj.com/KMDJ/News/NewsViewer.aspx?a=fa361fe0-40d2-45d7-ab25-1788d8425270)；定位：Paragraph 47.
   來源：[GIS expects revenue proportion for fingerprint recognition modules to rise to 30% in three years](https://www.digitimes.com/news/a20220311PD204/general-interface-solution-fingerprint-recognition.html)；定位：Paragraphs 64–69, especially paragraph 65.
3. `UNKNOWN` — GIS-KY supplies major AR/VR headset brands.
   期間：2025 annual-report disclosure; no named brand-customer period established.
   說明：GIS's annual report says it develops AR/VR products and cooperates with key customers in emerging applications, but does not name those customers. It separately names Apple, Meta, Sony, and Samsung as companies investing in the AR/VR industry; that market participation does not establish a GIS supplier relationship. The asserted customer scope, named brands, and period therefore remain unverified.
   來源：[General Interface Solution (GIS) Holding Limited — Annual Report 2025](https://financialfilings.com/filings/gis-6456/annual-report/2026/47935749/)；定位：Industry overview, page 87; marketing strategy, lines 2674–2679.

## 判定

Overall `PARTIAL`。

建議改寫：Historical reporting supports GIS-KY as a supplier of Apple's iPad touch modules and MacBook display modules through 2024, and identifies Samsung as a smartphone-related customer in 2020–2022 reporting. GIS's 2025 annual report describes AR/VR work with unnamed customers; it does not verify supply relationships with major headset brands.

來源限制：
- The named Apple and Samsung relationships are supported by secondary reporting rather than a current issuer disclosure naming those customers.
- The 2025 issuer annual report gives general product and customer descriptions but does not name Apple or Samsung as current customers.
- The annual report's mention of Apple, Meta, Sony, and Samsung as AR/VR market participants is not evidence that GIS supplies them.
- The original wording does not specify a date, product scope, or what qualifies as a “major” AR/VR brand.

Focused source review found historical support for the Apple and Samsung relationships, but no issuer-confirmed linkage between GIS and named major AR/VR headset brands. Further confirmation requires a customer-specific issuer disclosure or filing.
