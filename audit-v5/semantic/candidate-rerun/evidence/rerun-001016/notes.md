# 重驗筆記 — 正達 / 3149:material_named_relationship:03278f3ab45f2eff

原始 claim：[[Apple]] — 過往主要客戶

## 原子主張

1. `SUPPORTED` — Apple was a major customer of 正達 (G-Tech) in the past.
   期間：2011–2013
   說明：Historical reporting identifies 正達 as a major iPad cover-glass supplier and reports Apple-related product orders. A 2012 article estimated iPad protective-glass revenue could reach about 50% of 正達’s revenue that year; this was a forecast, not verified realized revenue. Another report gives iPad order-award shares for 2011–2013, not Apple’s share of 正達’s total revenue. The historical relationship and its materiality are supported, but not by an issuer disclosure naming Apple as a major customer.
   來源：[iPad觸控面板保護玻璃　7成高良率領先競爭者 — Smart自學網／Smart智富月刊](https://smart.businessweekly.com.tw/Magazine/detail.aspx?id=46231&p=2)；定位：Article body, sections describing 正達 as a major iPad protective-glass supplier and estimating iPad-glass revenue share.
   來源：[正達今年EPS估衰退 明年將大反彈 — 自由財經](https://ec.ltn.com.tw/article/paper/640965)；定位：Article body, paragraph citing Yuanta’s report on iPad orders and iMac shipments.
2. `CONTRADICTED` — Apple was only a former customer, meaning the Apple supply relationship had ended.
   期間：2025 presentation; status after that period not established
   說明：The issuer’s 2025 presentation describes 正達 as Apple Track Pad’s leading supplier by shipments and says it accounted for about 70% of Apple laptop/iPad demand. This conflicts with reading「過往」as asserting the relationship had already ended, but does not establish the relationship’s status after the presentation.
   來源：[PowerPoint 演示文稿 — 正達國際光電（G-TECH）](https://www.gtoc.com.tw/upload/cominfo/202506261606150.pdf)；定位：Slide headed「正達為Apple Track Pad 出貨量第一之供應商」.

## 判定

Overall `PARTIAL`。

建議改寫：正達 historically had a material Apple-related business, including iPad protective glass; its 2025 issuer presentation also described it as an Apple Track Pad supplier. The phrase「過往主要客戶」is ambiguous if intended to mean the relationship had ended.

來源限制：
- Historical evidence found is secondary reporting, not an issuer filing that names Apple as a major customer.
- Historical order shares and revenue estimates are not interchangeable with Apple’s share of 正達’s total sales.
- The 2025 issuer presentation does not establish Apple relationship status after the period it describes.

Found evidence supporting a historical material relationship and an issuer disclosure inconsistent with treating it as definitively discontinued; no reliable evidence located establishing the relationship’s status after 2025.
