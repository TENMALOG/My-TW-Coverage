## 判定

Overall `PARTIAL`。

建議改寫：A June 21, 2024 secondary report described Hon Hai as holding approximately 25% of AMAX and characterized increased component procurement and technical/product cooperation as expected or under discussion. AMAX’s 2025 annual report reports that two entities in the Foxconn Industrial Internet ownership chain held 18.64% combined as of April 7, 2026. The current depth and implementation of procurement or technical collaboration remain unverified.

來源限制：
- The 2025 annual report source is an issuer report translation hosted by a third-party filing site; the report identifies the issuer and the relevant shareholding date.
- The 2024 approximately 25% figure is historical secondary reporting and should not be carried forward as a current percentage.
- The available procurement disclosure masks supplier names; it cannot confirm or disprove Hon Hai-specific purchasing.
- The evidence for technical cooperation concerns potential cooperation and plans under discussion, not quantified implementation.

Stopped after focused searches and review of the issuer’s 2025 annual-report content, an official Hon Hai reporting page, and the 2024 secondary report. The current equity figure is verifiable; current Hon Hai-specific procurement scale and implemented technical-cooperation scope remain unresolved.
