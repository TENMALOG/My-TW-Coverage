原始 claim：[[Vestas]] (唯一客戶, 2025終止)

## 原子主張

1. `UNKNOWN` — Vestas was 天力離岸’s only customer.
   期間：The claim does not specify a period or whether it means all company business or blade manufacturing. Issuer disclosures report FY2023 and FY2024 revenue concentration.
   說明：The issuer’s filing calls Vestas a major customer and reports that it accounted for 98% and 94% of consolidated revenue in 2023 and 2024, respectively. That establishes substantial concentration, not sole-customer exclusivity or the customer scope. Secondary reporting also describes Vestas as a single customer, but does not establish that it was the company’s only customer.

## 判定

Overall `PARTIAL`。

建議改寫：天力離岸於2025年5月22日完成Vestas已下訂的309支葉片生產，並公告將結束與Vestas的合作。Vestas於2023及2024年分別占天力離岸合併營收約98%及94%；現有證據未能確認其為天力離岸唯一客戶。

來源限制：
- The available issuer announcement is reproduced by financial-news websites; a direct MOPS/TPEx filing URL was not verified.
- The disclosed 94% and 98% figures are shares of consolidated revenue, not customer counts.
- Ending cooperation with 天力離岸’s Vestas group does not establish that Vestas ended all business or operations in Taiwan.

Targeted searches located the issuer announcement and contemporaneous reporting. They support the 2025 end of cooperation and high revenue concentration, but do not resolve whether Vestas was the sole customer across the company or within a defined business scope.
