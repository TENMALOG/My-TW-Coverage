## 判定

Overall `PARTIAL`。

建議改寫：Kanto-PPC was listed by TSMC as a major worldwide chemical supplier in 2017 and received a TSMC chemical-material quality cooperation award in 2019. The reviewed evidence does not establish that it currently supplies TSMC with electronic-grade sulfuric acid, or that it is the exclusive supplier.

來源限制：
- The TSMC disclosures support a historical general chemical-supplier relationship, not sulfuric-acid-specific supply.
- Kanto-PPC's and the government report's descriptions of sulfuric-acid capability do not identify TSMC as the customer.
- A 2025 secondary report describes a TSMC supply-chain relationship and planned future U.S. production, but does not document current electronic-grade sulfuric-acid deliveries or exclusivity.

Focused searches found historical general supplier evidence and evidence of Kanto-PPC's product capability, but no reliable source resolving current, product-specific supply or sole-source status. Those atoms remain UNKNOWN rather than being inferred from capability, general supplier listings, or planned future production.
