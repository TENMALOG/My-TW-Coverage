## 判定

Overall `PARTIAL`。

建議改寫：凌網 offers the HyRead electronic-book platform, the HyLib library-automation system, and RFID self-service book borrowing and return machines. The claim that HyRead is No. 1 by market share remains unverified.

來源限制：
- Issuer product pages support the existence of the named offerings but are not independent market-ranking evidence.
- University-library adoption coverage, library market share, and an overall electronic-book platform ranking are distinct measures.
- The available secondary market-share statements do not establish a defined period, geography, denominator, or ranking methodology for the exact HyRead No. 1 assertion.

Research limit reached after focused searches and source checks. The product-offering atoms are supported; the HyRead market-rank atom remains unresolved because the claim’s market scope, period, and denominator are unspecified and no matching reliable evidence was found.
