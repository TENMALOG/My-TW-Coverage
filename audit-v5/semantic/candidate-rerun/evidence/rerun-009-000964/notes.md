## 判定

Overall `PARTIAL`。

建議改寫：Wistron classifies AI Compute—including GPU servers, baseboards, and modules—as a growth platform and stated in March 2024 that it was increasing deliveries of NVIDIA DGX systems. The reviewed evidence does not establish that Wistron is among NVIDIA's most critical suppliers or that it exclusively supplies DGX baseboards and related compute modules across an unspecified period.

來源限制：
- The issuer presentation supports product categorization, not NVIDIA-specific market share, exclusivity, or the claim that GPU baseboards are a core business.
- The 2023 exclusivity report is secondary and limited to specified GPU generations; it cannot establish exclusivity for all DGX systems or later periods.
- No ranking metric, supplier comparison set, module definition, or market denominator is provided for the superlative and exclusivity claims.

Reviewed issuer disclosures and focused contemporaneous supply-chain reporting. The remaining claims lack the scope, period, or denominator needed to verify them; broader research would not cure those ambiguities without more specific claim wording or primary evidence.
