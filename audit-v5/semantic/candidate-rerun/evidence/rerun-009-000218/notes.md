## 判定

Overall `PARTIAL`。

建議改寫：匯僑設計將國際精品列為服務領域，並披露多個亞洲精品零售專案案例；公開資料未能核實其對 LVMH、Kering、Richemont 旗下品牌的長期服務關係，也未能核實其台灣精品零售空間市佔率逾 80%。

來源限制：
- Issuer presentations are primary disclosures but their project lists do not establish relationship duration or market-wide denominators.
- The >80% figure found in secondary reporting is described as coverage, not methodologically defined market share.
- Luxury-group websites establish current brand affiliations; they do not establish historical ownership at the date of every issuer project.

Research exhausted at the eight-turn limit. Official issuer materials support luxury-retail activity and named project examples, but no reliable source located establishes long-term group-wide relationships or a defined Taiwan market-share measure above 80%.
