## 判定

Overall `PARTIAL`。

建議改寫：麗升能源科技股份有限公司（8087；LeadSun Greentech Corporation）前身為華鎂鑫科技股份有限公司；股東於2024年5月24日通過更名案，並於2024年6月7日完成公司名稱變更登記。

來源限制：
- The May 24 approval-date evidence is a secondary reproduction of a MOPS announcement; the June 7 registration date and prior name are confirmed by the official MOEA registry.
- The TPEX notice concerns the later replacement of shares and start of trading under the renamed company; it is not the source for the May approval date.

Official registry and TPEX records resolve the distinction between the May shareholder approval and the June registered name change; no unresolved material atom remains.
