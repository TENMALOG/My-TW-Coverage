# 重驗筆記 — 台達電 / 2308:objective_ranking_or_market_share:102dbd9ad2f63ed0

原始 claim：台達電 (2308) 是全球最大的[[交換式電源供應器]]製造商，以自有品牌 DELTA 行銷全球。

## 原子主張

1. `UNKNOWN` — 台達電是全球最大的交換式電源供應器製造商。
   期間：Present-tense claim; no specific ranking period is stated.
   說明：Delta's 2010 CSR report described the Group as the world's largest provider of switching power supplies, but this is a historical company statement, not an independently substantiated current ranking, and “provider” does not specifically establish “manufacturer.” Delta's 2024 annual report describes leadership in power management and thermal solutions but gives no comparative rank for switching power supplies. No defined market scope or ranking denominator (such as revenue, units, or merchant supply) was established.
   來源：[Delta Group CSR Report 2010](https://filecenter.deltaww.com/about/download/2010_Delta_CSR_Report_EN.pdf)；定位：Delta Group Overview, report page 12
   來源：[DELTA ELECTRONICS, INC. 2024 ANNUAL REPORT](https://filecenter.deltaww.com/IR/download/annual_report/2024annual.pdf)；定位：Letter to Shareholders, subsection “Power Electronics”
2. `SUPPORTED` — 台達電以自有品牌 DELTA 行銷全球。
   期間：The brand's stated inauguration year is 2010; own-brand development is also described in April 2024.
   說明：Official company materials identify Delta as its own brand and state that a Brand Office was established to manage global brand activities. This supports global brand promotion, but does not establish availability in every individual country.
   來源：[Delta Group CSR Report 2010](https://filecenter.deltaww.com/about/download/2010_Delta_CSR_Report_EN.pdf)；定位：Delta Group Overview, report page 12
   來源：[Implementing sales platforms and facilitating cross-domain integration](https://brandnews.deltaww.com/EN/InsightsDetail/12534)；定位：Section “Building regional capabilities, integrating global operations”

## 判定

Overall `PARTIAL`。

建議改寫：台達電自2010年起發展Delta自有品牌，並設立品牌辦公室管理全球品牌活動。

來源限制：
- The “world's largest” wording appears as Delta's own historical claim; current official materials reviewed do not provide a comparative ranking, market-share measurement, or defined denominator.
- Global brand-management activity supports global promotion but does not prove distribution or marketing in every country.

Focused searches of official company materials and current ranking references did not resolve the present-tense global-largest claim with a defined market scope and denominator. It remains UNKNOWN; lack of resolving evidence is not treated as evidence that the ranking is false.
