# QA Sampling Policy

Version: `sampling-1-draft`

Low-risk carry-forward data remains subject to independent sampling.

Initial default: sample at least 5% of each meaningful low-risk cohort, stratified where practical by sector, source family, migration origin and claim type.

Sampling outcomes are separate QA observations; not being sampled is not equivalent to manual verification.

Escalation guidance:

- isolated non-critical failure: repair and increase local sample;
- repeated same-pattern failure: expand to the affected source/policy/sector cohort;
- identity contamination or systematic mapping error: pause the cohort and escalate to root adjudication.

The percentage is a starting operational rule and may be changed only by a versioned policy update with recorded rationale.
